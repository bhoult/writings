# When and what made you decide to be a minimalist?

They say you should not bite the hand that feeds you. It follows that you should not feed the mouth that bites.

Consumerism bit me too many times. It's promises are irresistible. In the end it always bites. You will find debt is a mad beast, friendly until it turns. Hopefully you survive.

Possessions are the nest we build to make our world small. Safe, comfortable, reliable but oh so very small. In the end it is all taken away regardless. Dare not look at the life you sacrificed when that time comes.
