# What are the key differences between NutriBullet, Vitamix, and Blendtec high-speed blenders? What are their speeds and how tough are their engines? Is Nutribullet aiming to be part of each and every household in the Western world?

I got the original nutribullet. It worked well for smoothies and was simple to clean. Very convenient. After a year both the bases wore out (the blade part not the motor). By that time the more powerful 900 was out, so I got that. It was slightly better at blending worked well for most things. I was growing a lot of blackberries by this time though and it would not blend blackberry seeds or things like coconut meat to a smooth consistency. I used the 900 for over a year and whatever issues they had with the base wearing out seem to have been fixed.

A week ago a friend gave me a vitamix 5200. I was previously thrilled with the nutribullet, but I have to say that the Vitamix is superior in every respect. It blends coconut into coconut cream. Pulverizes blackberry seeds until they are unnoticeable. I considered the lack of a tamper on the nutribullet to be one less thing to clean, but it also meant that smoothies are about all that the nutribullet is good for. The tamper allows you to make nut butters, dough, ice-cream. The nutribullet is in a sealed container so you can't mix hot liquids without it exploding. The Vitamix can not only mix hot liquids, but if you let it run long enough will heat the liquids itself.... which is another difference. You can only run the nutribullet a minute or so, but can leave the vitamix running as long as it takes.

The nutribullet is slightly easier to clean, but you basically clean both the same way, add water with a drop of soap and blend. If I had known how much better and more versatile the vitamix was I would have spend the extra $250 (reconditioned vitamix cost) long ago. It is definitely worth it and has allowed me to get rid of my food processor and coffee grinder as well. If all you want is smoothies and don't care about occasional seeds and grit then the nutribullet is fine for that. Maybe the new RX does a better job breaking those down, but the range of possibilities available by using the tamper still outweighs the cost benefit in my opinion.

If the vitamix were to break tomorrow I would buy another one immediately.
