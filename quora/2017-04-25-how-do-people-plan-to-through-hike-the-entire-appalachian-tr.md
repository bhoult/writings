# How do people plan to through-hike the entire Appalachian Trail?

In addition to what others have said, I would suggest getting a few books about hiking the AT, to get an idea what it will be like so you will be better able to plan.

Brysons “A walk in the woods” is a pretty good one, although he never finished the hike. Personally, “Walking on the happy side of misery” was my favorite.

Other than that try to make sure your pack is as light as possible. You should easily be able to get it under 30lbs. This is probably the most important factor in determining how much you will enjoy the hike.
