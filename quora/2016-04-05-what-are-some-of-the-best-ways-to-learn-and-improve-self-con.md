# What are some of the best ways to learn and improve self-control?

Delay satisfaction for the things you want and force action on the things you avoid. In other words practice.

Hungry? Wait a few hours. Want to buy something? Do it next week. Want to watch tv? Wait an hour. Make a list of rules like this, make it unambiguous and set consequences for failure. Did you not wait to eat? Then you have to fast the next day. Did you impulse buy? Then you have to donate double the amount to charity.

Make a list of the things you don't want to do but should. Make yourself do one each day or each week. Set consequences if you don't.

The key in all this is to always accept the consequences of failure. You are allowed to fail but you know it will cost you. Success leads to positive reinforcement, failure leads to negative reinforcement. As it should be.

The reason self discipline is hard is because we can usually cheat and get away with it. If you are rewarded by failure and punished by success then you will be fighting your subconscious on every decision. For a time you have to artificially force yourself to be accountable and aware of your actions. You have to make yourself in some way deal with the consequences.
