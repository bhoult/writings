# How can I keep myself from wanting to buy new clothes and material things?

There is probably no easy fix for wanting something. You will stop wanting it naturally when you see that it is worthless.

The real skill to develop is not doing what you want, but instead doing what you should. Of course what you should do is determined by what you want out of life… so it is a bit circular. However the principal is to remaintain true to long term goals by foregoing short term desires that would detract.

I don't think there are any tricks to this. You get better by practice. Each time you resist it will become easier, each time you give in it will get harder. Just keep your eyes on the goal.
