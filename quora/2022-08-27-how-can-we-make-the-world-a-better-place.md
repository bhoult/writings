# How can we make the world a better place?

I saw a small insect. Much the same as those you too have seen and ignored. But on this occasion I lay on the earth and followed its path, I saw it intricately through a lens. I noticed the iridescence of its exoskeleton, the precision and speed of its movements, the tenacity of its purpose.

The insect was glorious, certainly “better”, and yet it was unchanged.

Notice what is better in the world, and it will be a better place.
