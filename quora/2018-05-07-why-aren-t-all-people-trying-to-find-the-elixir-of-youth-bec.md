# Why aren't all people trying to find the elixir of youth, because whatever you are doing in your life you are going to die?

What you are really asking I suppose is why people are willing to accept their own death. A few reasons:

We don't know where we came from before we had life. We don't know where we are going after. We don't even know much about where or who or what we are now. It is not productive to worry too much about an unknown.

Worrying about something we can't do anything about prevents us from enjoying what we do have. In a practical sense you will never experience non-living. So eternity so far as you will experience is the span of your life divided into infinite moments. The idea that life goes on after you are gone is just an abstract concept that you will never experience.

If we could live forever or even somewhat longer then life would become more hellish than we can imagine. The earth is finite. After we reach the maximum carrying capacity at which we can continue to exist then we will be destroying the very thing that sustains us. We may have reached that point already. If we did not die then we could also not continue to reproduce without killing everything, and then ourselves.

The life you have was a free gift that you did not earn. Were you able to take more it would be at the expense of others. This may be the very nature of evil.
