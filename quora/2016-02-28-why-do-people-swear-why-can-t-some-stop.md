# Why do people swear? Why can't some stop?

Swearing is the adult equivalent of a screaming child in a grocery store. It is the bold announcement that you have nothing useful to say on a topic and yet insist on babbling a string of nonsense because you require more attention than is merited. It exists for the same reason a monkey will defecate in his hand and hurl it at the cause of its anger. It knows it is impotent to accomplish anything useful but it lacks the self control to restrain itself from making the attempt. It is a tool to escalate a bad situation in the hope that those around you will either recognize your frustration or become similarly frustrated. It is a warning that circumstances have exceeded your ability to cope with them and you are about to devolve into a reactionary wailing juvenile.

People notice it for the same reason they try to glimpse broken bodies in car wrecks or celebrity scandals in tabloids. But for many any kind of attention is good attention. I expect some can't stop because they are either too lazy or not intelligent enough to formulate a considered response. Those around them probably know this and have become accustomed to ignoring them. Then they have to throw a tantrum to get attention and motivate someone else to solve their issue.

Swearing is a useful indicator that the person doing it would be best avoided. It would be even more convenient if they had "incompetent" tattooed on their foreheads, or perhaps a "recent invertebrate" t-shirt. Until then we will have to rely on the nonsense that they inevitably spew from their mouths. The battle cry of the imbecile.
