# If you are told that you can only spend $7 USD on food for one week (in the USA), what would you buy, assuming that you start now?

I would buy $5 of rice, beans, flour, oil. Bread is cheaper if you make it yourself. I would use the other $2 to buy some seeds. Leafy greens like spinach or lettuce can be harvested in a couple weeks. The second week I would do the same except I would add some sweet potatoes which have edible greens when planted. The third week I would buy a live chicken that produces eggs.

During this time I would forrage as much as possible, dandylion greens, lambs quarters, grass seeds, I would go to a local library and find all the edible wild food growing in my area. I would also drink a lot of pine tea for the vitamin c. I would make a slingshot and hunt for rabbits and birds. I would make snares and traps along the path wherever I go. I would fish whenever possible.

In the US I would be dumpster diving at grocery stores and offering to wash dishes in exchange for food in restaurants.
