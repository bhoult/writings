# Can a human win in a battle with a tiger barehanded?

I once met a one armed man in Kenya that had killed a lion barehanded. He had shoved the now missing arm down the lions throat and held on while the lion clawed at this stomach and bit his arm. Eventually it choked to death.

The man then walked 8 kilometers holding his intestines in his remaining arm.

He wore a necklace made out of lion teeth around his neck and nobody made fun of his one arm or the scars that covered his body.
