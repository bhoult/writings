# Does mutation have any scientific base? Is it true that mutation can induce super-natural powers in normal humans? Any example of mutant?

Genetics are basically instructions for how to build a biological organism. In this case people. As such it is much like the recipe for making a cake. A mutation would be like randomly inserting some substance in some random quantity into the recipe.

In most cases given all the possible substances it will make the cake worse (dirt, soap, sawdust, motor-oil etc.) In many other cases it may make it lethal (rat poison, uranium, anti-freeze). In a very few cases it may make it better (nutmeg, cinnamon, ginger) if it also happens to be in a reasonable quantity.

When people try to improve the recipe, they make educated guesses and if it happens to be an improvement they might add it to the recipe (genetic engineering). When nature does it, it is totally random but if it does a billion random things and a few of them result in something having a slightly better chance of breeding and those things actually do breed successfully then there might be a small change (like lactose tolerance mentioned by Vaibhav). Needless to say this takes a long time (millions of years) and is at the cost of millions of non-beneficial mutations (down syndrome for example).

It is unlikely that it would result in any "super-natural" powers in the same way that adding gasoline to your cake mix would not make it able to shoot laser beams from some cakey orifice. It would probably be something more like having an abundance of nostril hair. Consider that no known humans have "super-natural" power, but also no known animals that we know of have it either. It would be much more likely for house flies to develop telekinesis than people since there are so much more of them and their lifespan is so much shorter and as a result they mutate much faster. Actually bacteria with super-powers would by now be common if it were possible at all.

In the end you have biology, chemistry, and physics defining what is even possible and genetics trying to optimize within that framework. Super powers are like giving someone three legos and a rubber band and asking them to make a working space shuttle.
