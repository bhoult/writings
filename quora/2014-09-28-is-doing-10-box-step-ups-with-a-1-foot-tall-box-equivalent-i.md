# Is doing 10 box step-ups with a 1-foot tall box equivalent in energy expenditure to walking up a 10-foot hill?

Not that I am an expert at this or anything, but I expect energy wise the box steps would be equivalent to walking up a 10 foot hill then down again. With each step you go both up and down whereas with a hill you just go up.

The muscles used are probably different as well. With a hill the ground is not level so you will use your calves more to compensate. But on overall energy expenditure I expect steps will use more than a hill (if you just go up the hill).
