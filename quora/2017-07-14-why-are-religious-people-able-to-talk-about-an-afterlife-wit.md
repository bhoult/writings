# Why are religious people able to talk about an afterlife without offending people, but when I claim I don't believe in it people are offended?

I have done a lot of hiking / backpacking in my life. On long hikes you meet a lot of people doing the same thing you are doing. Naturally when you talk it is about things that you have in common, the food, the weather, the gear you are using etc. You all have a common goal so you encourage each other to get up, to start walking, to not quit.

Now imagine if someone showed up and said what you were doing was stupid. You should quit and go do something more reasonable like watching TV. Why are you spending all this effort when you could just drive there?

Maybe you tune him out and keep going, but he just keeps talking… eventually he declares that you are mentally incompetent and starts telling your friend he should just quit and go get a hamburger.

Kind of annoying right? And this is just people who are doing something they like for no particular reason. Not people who have based most of their life on a decision made long ago, and who have sacrificed a great deal to get where they are.

The same applies to a religious person who insists on trying to persuade another group of people, or to a salesperson cold calling someone eating dinner with their family. They have nothing to contribute but insist on contributing it anyway.

When people have decided where they want to go they will welcome people with the same direction. When people try to convince them they are wrong they will find it annoying. When they keep being annoyed they will likely get angry.

If people are talking about the afterlife and you state that you don't believe in it then you are basically saying that you have nothing to contribute to what they find meaningful. What is the point of talking after this? Why would they listen?

You are basically saying “This conversation is stupid, and you are all stupid for having it, and your friends and relatives that convinced you of this are also stupid. And I know better than all of you.”. Try going to a new age festival and telling them that their crystals and symbols are imaginary. Or a ancient aliens convention and stating that aliens don't exist.
