# Hiking: are trekking poles worth it?

I am going to go against the trend here and say that they are kind of overrated. If you like them them by all means go ahead and get some, but they can generally be replaced by a piece of wood or bamboo. 

If you are going a long distance or if you have a heavy pack then some kind of support is helpful as had been mentioned. With a pack your center of gravity is higher and you will not be as stable as you are used to so a stick or pole will help you catch your balance if you trip over a root or something. Also it is nice to help lift yourself on ascents and keep traction on descents. 

Personally I always used a large 6ft long hiking staff. I tried the poles for a bit and then went back to the staff. I like to hold it high by my shoulder and lean against it to catch my breath periodically. I also like having one hand free in case I need to grab something.

If possible try both, borrow some poles to test, and then try it with a staff made of wood or bamboo. See which you prefer.
