# How can I sleep in hot water?

Look up isolation tank or isolation chamber.. here is a link: Isolation tank

People sleep in these things by essentially adding an enormous amount of epsom salt so you just kind of float on top like the dead sea. 

If the isolation part does not appeal to you then leave the lid open.
