# From a scientifc standpoint, what do you think will happen to our consciousness after we die? Without involving religion?

The scientific method (Wikipedia):

Define a questionGather information and resources (observe)Form an explanatory hypothesisTest the hypothesis by performing an experiment and collecting data in a reproducible mannerAnalyze the dataInterpret the data and draw conclusions that serve as a starting point for new hypothesisPublish resultsRetest (frequently done by other scientists)Stuff that is “scientific” is derived from the above method, which requires observation, then testing, then repeatability. Nobody has repeatedly reported back post death. If they have then we may have to redefine the definition of dead.

If someone comes up with a “Scientific” hypothesis for an unknown with no data by means of handwaving and pseudoscientific gibberish. Then they are attempting to redefine science into something that agrees with their own newly minted religion.

If you want to disregard religion as a legitimate source of knowledge then you are going to have to accept that a great deal of human experience will remain unknowable to you.
