# How do you sleep in the woods without a tent?

If you are tired enough you just lie on the ground and sleep. If you get lucky you find a rock overhang and make sure there are no snakes. If it is cold you try to make a fire against a reflector of some sort. If you have time you make a shelter out of branches rocks and leaves. If you don't then you might just bury yourself in leaves and hope it does not rain.

You will probably sleep poorly. Bugs will bite you, the ground is hard and cold. You will be itchy, sore and cold.

In general though, if you can't sleep then you just keep walking into the dark. Eventually when you are tired enough, how you sleep won't be a problem.
