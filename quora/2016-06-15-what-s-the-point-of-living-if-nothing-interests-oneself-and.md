# What's the point of living if nothing interests oneself and nothing makes one happy?

I think this state is most common among people who are both self centered and bored with themselves. Either that or they are suffering from depression as others have mentioned. The solution is to develop empathy this lets you find meaning in the needs of others, since you lack meaning in yourself. Basically you are at a point where you don't see the purpose in anything you do, this is a first world problem. If you were truly suffering then that would motivate you to change. As it is if you succeed you will be relatively comfortable, if you fail you will still be comfortable... you have given up hope on trying anything substantial because you expect you will fail, and you fear failure.

My suggestion is to practice failure, to seek out pain, to make yourself uncomfortable. Give up the things that act as your security blanket, experience fear and vulnerability. You will find satisfaction in overcoming these fears, you will recognize the things you have overcome in others and will find further satisfaction in helping them.

For myself I decided to leave my unsatisfying comfort zone and hike the Appalachian trail for six months. It did not solve all my problems, but it was the first step in climbing out of my own rut. It gave me perspective and time to reflect on who I was and what I wanted out of life. The point is you are not happy with where you are and who you are, so go somewhere different and unfamiliar so you can make new patterns, and become someone different.
