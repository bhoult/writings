# How can I build a minimalist style website?

I am not sure I am qualified to answer this. I strive toward a minimalist lifestyle and am a web developer, but I am not especially artistic. When you refer to a minimalist style I assume you mean an artistic look. I am not an expert on that. 

But since you asked, my answer would be to simply leave out all but the essential elements on each page. I would consider Quora's design to be minimalist. Simply focus on what you want to communicate and remove the clutter.
