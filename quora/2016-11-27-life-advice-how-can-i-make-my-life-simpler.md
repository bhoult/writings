# Life Advice: How can I make my life simpler?

Control of the future is complicated. Each decision branching off into multiple potentials, and there far in the distance is the one you want. It shines like a beacon, surrounded by a fog of undefinable darkness, the futures you fear.

How will you traverse this space? Will you gain the strength to push through? That is hard. Will you study to find the wisdom to know the way, avoiding all the hidden traps and obstacles? That is hard. Will you make alliances to help you on your journey? Will you find someone you can follow to the future that is most similar to what you want, or will you dominate others and use them as sacrifices along the way. Is there a price that will have to be paid? Will you accept it and do what has to be done or will you trick others into paying it for you? Will you take their bright spark of longing and use it to fuel your journey. Or will you be used and consumed by others?

If you finally arrive, will there be enough of you left to still want what you find? Will you be disappointed? Will you forever want something else, a pilgrimage in endless circles until you drop dead? Will you long to be who you were before the journey wore away your innocence?

Simple life is easy… food, water, air, shelter. Do not be deceived, do not deceive yourself, you will end at the same place you started. Contentment is hard.
