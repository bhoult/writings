# What would be the effect of being shot with rock salt bullets from a Desert Eagle gun?

I am not an expert on this, but I suspect it would be about the same as shooting a half teaspoon of powdered sugar from a slingshot. The force would shatter the salt bullet and the powder would fly out the end in a small poof. The bang should be satisfying though.

I know you can do something similar with a shotgun but I believe they encase the salt in a plastic sheath and use a smaller charge. That way some of the pieces hit hard enough at close range to break the skin. If it does break the skin then being salt in a wound it will sting a lot.
