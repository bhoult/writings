# How can one make money starting a blog?

Minimalintentions.com, blog about minimalism and reducing lifestyle expenses. It had been up about three years, 20 something articles of unique content gets about 25 visitors a day on average and makes about $50/year on adsense. 

There are a couple of articles on there on this exact subject with more details. I suspect you could make more if you consistently write more articles. I tend to post only thing that I consider significant which happens infrequently....unfortunately.
