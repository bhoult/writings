# What is the loneliest number, and why?

I have heard that it is one, but I cannot believe that to be true. One is too self centered to think of others. Assuming he is even aware of them. 

But consider poor infinity, its nearest neighbor is still infinitely its inferior. Even negative infinity is at least admired for its humility. But poor infinity knows it is lonely at the top... infinitely so. 

Then there are the irrational numbers, but of course nobody trusts them. Who cares how they feel?

Does three feel guilt for having sacrificed one and two for his own existence? When his time comes and he is finally consumed by seven, will he feel relief or will he be dragged screaming toward his assimilation?

And how about Bacon... nobody will even acknowledge that he is a number.
