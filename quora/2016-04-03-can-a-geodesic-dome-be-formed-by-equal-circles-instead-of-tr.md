# Can a geodesic dome be formed by equal circles instead of triangles?

That would certainly simplify construction if you could, but I don't think it is possible. You would end up with something like honeycomb which makes a flat surface instead of a dome. You may want to look into the "star dome" design which lets you use flexible materials without joints like pvc or bamboo.
