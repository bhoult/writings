# Which food is the best to take for sustained energy while hiking?

Pretty much the exact opposite of what you should eat in a normal sedentary life, with the addition of weight being an important consideration.

If you check the package, then look for maximum calories / fat / carbs per gram. Of course cost is also a factor for most as well.

On the Appalachian trail this resulted in some interesting combinations. Like Snickers soup which was a bunch of candy bars and gorp all in a ziplock bag which had melted in the heat. Olive oil mixed with Gatorade powder, drank by the mouth full before every thousand foot elevation gain.

One guy calculated that the optimal strategy was to buy the cheapest hotdogs and beer at each town which he would carry into the closest woods and consume until he passed out.

Realistically though, mountain House freeze dried meals are great if you can afford them. Peanut butter and Fig Newtons are surprisingly calorie dense. Lipton sides or instant mashed potatoes ramen noodles or rice are good choices. Squeeze butter or Olive oil are good for increasing calories. Anything that you can just add water to make is ideal.

Powdered Gatorade or other sugary powdered drink mixes are good to keep your energy level up as you walk. Hot chocolate or powdered coffee is great when you are not walking.

Pig out in towns that you pass through or at the end of your hike.
