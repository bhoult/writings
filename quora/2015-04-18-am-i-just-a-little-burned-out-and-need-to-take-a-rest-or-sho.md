# Am I just a little burned out and need to take a rest, or should I suck it up and push on?

The syntax does not need to stick in your head after a few weeks. I have used about 14 different programming languages. At this point the only one still stuck in my head is the last one I was using. In the "good old days" we had books that we constantly referred to as references. Things were simpler then. You could memorize all the keywords in a language and all the library functions and feel quite clever about yourself. 

Now there are so many libraries, patterns, frameworks that you can never memorize them all. It would do you no good if you did as they would have all changed by then.

Now the skill to have is the ability to look things up and put them to use without even really understanding them at all. If you do it enough then you get faster. Eventually you may consider yourself an expert in some small area. Then you move on and learn something new, forget something old and hopefully retain the principle if not the syntax.

In software the learning never ends, so you never "arrive".
