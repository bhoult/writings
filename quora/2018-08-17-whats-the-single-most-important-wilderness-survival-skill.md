# What’s the single most important wilderness survival skill?

The ability to not panic. Panic robs you of all the other skills on which you will need to rely. Humility is probably second, you need an accurate estimate of your abilities vs what you will face. If you screw this up it will lead to injury and possible death. Adaptability is probably next, it is easier to change your expectations than it is to change the world
