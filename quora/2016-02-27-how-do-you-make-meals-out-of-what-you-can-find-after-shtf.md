# How do you make meals out of what you can find after shtf?

I expect after all the food stores are used up then you won't be "making meals". You will just eat what you find whenever you get it. With that in mind, pine trees are edible... At least the parts you can swallow, inner bark needles etc. Grass seeds are edible but it would take a long time to collect them. Insects, worms, mice, rats, dogs and cats are edible, you may think that you will become an expert deer hunter or fisherman...so does everybody else.

There are a lot more tasty edible plants, but you will have to learn them in advance since surviving the end of the world is a full time job and the internet will probably not be working. You may also have to consider that once they get hungry enough some will be wondering how to make a meal out of you.
