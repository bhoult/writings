# How do I know if I really need to buy something or if it’s just impulse buying?

I used to have a rule that for every $100 that something cost I would have to wait a week before buying it. There was a two month limit so if I had to buy a car I would not be walking for a year.

Most of the time I forgot what it was I wanted before the time had elapsed … the impulse had passed.
