# What are the best things to discuss with a great mentor and how?

Discuss your mentors biggest mistakes. A mentor will tell you about this in the hopes that you will avoid them and thereby exceed his/her own accomplishments. If they do not then they are not a mentor they just want you as an admirer. It has been said that people will wish you the best so long as it is not better than what they have. This is not true of a mentor.

Understand why they made the mistake. Every mistake was believed to be a good idea by a rational person before it became a mistake. Something was overlooked or pridefully ignored, a lie was discovered a piece of truth exposed. These things come with pain are worthy of study. Learn what they did to correct it, or endure it. This made them stronger, be grateful if they offer you the source of their strength. Don't take it lightly.

Many will offer you stories of their success if only to distract you from noticing the depth of their failures. A mentor will lie down in the muck so you can walk on his back and remain clean a little longer... to reach a little further... to mentor another.
