# Are you surprised when someone tells you they believe in God?

Two friends stand at the edge of the world and gaze into the abyss. Thiest turns to Atheist and says “This abyss continues forever I think. It is as though I can see just a small part of something so much greater. I think I can see patterns that hint at a vastness and beauty I am unable to comprehend. We should study this so that we can eventually transcend the limitations of this small place that has caused us such pain.”

Atheist looks for a time and says “My friend, I see no order here. It is just chaos and emptiness. Let us study instead this dirt upon which we stand. By our own strength we can transform this prison into a paradise.”

Thiest: “It is not enough. It will never be enough. No matter how beautifully you arrange the dirt, no matter how comfortable this prison. How can I ever be satisfied while in these shadows I see brief flashes of beauty beyond anything that can ever exist in this small place?”

Atheist “What you see is your own hope projected upon the infinite. Tell me about the beauty of which you dream and I will build it here where we stand.”

Thiest: “Were it just a dream I could dismiss it, but I see it clearly and can not pretend otherwise. You are a great builder but you can only build with what you have. It will be beautiful but can never be more than a small reminder of what I see. Look again, can you not see it?”

Athiest: “I cant see what is not there, I cant build with shadows that cannot be held and measured. Would you rather I tell you otherwise?”

Thiest: “I wish that you could see. It is beautiful, but distracting. I neglect this place that you tend with such care. But tell me only truth, I would not ask you to pretend to be what you are not. Would you have me stay silent?”

Athiest: “No my friend, although I cannot see your visions as objective reality, they are an expression of what you hope for, a guide I can use to build a future in which we are able to coexist in peace.”

And there were no insults and fighting on that day. Their experiences diverged and as a result so did the beliefs that grew from those experiences. Later would come the arguments, hatred and wars, but the blood on the earth would testify to a mutually chosen blindness rather than different perspectives on a shared vision.
