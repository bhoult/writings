# Without any pro level skill on Web design and Graphic design can I get a good career in Digital marketing and freelancing?

If I roll a dice can I get a 6? If I try can I succeed? Yes!

If you are unskilled then your chances are low. Improve them by becoming more skilled. It is all about the odds. Either improve the odds or play a lot of games. The odds of success if you never try are zero. If you try badly then it is probably still low but infinitely better than zero.

I heard about a truck driver that kept applying for a programming job every chance he got. He did not know anything so he bombed the interview, but he always asked what he needed to improve to get the job. Then he went and studied that for a while. Improved the odds, then tried again. 20 something interviews later he was no longer a truck driver.

Quora seems littered with questions like this, is it possible? Can I succeed? I assume they want someone to tell them that it is easier than they think, so they can find the confidence to try. That is all B.S. You will probably fail badly. For some reason modern society has taught us it is shameful to try and fail. Like it is somehow preferable to spend your life fearfully quivering in some safe hole and never attempt to try any hard thing.

The older I become I realize that the most powerful thing you can learn in life is how to fail well. Fail fearlessly, fail gloriously and often. Brag about your failures to those too timid to try. Dont be ashamed of failing, it is like a door that does not open when you knock. Keep knocking, then kicking, then get a hammer. The barriers in your life will learn to fear you.
