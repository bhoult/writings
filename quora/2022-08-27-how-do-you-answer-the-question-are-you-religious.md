# How do you answer the question: Are you religious?

If you believe you know things that are unknowable, that you truly know anything, then, however much it may cause you offence, you are religious. If you take the further step of anthropomorphizing that delusion, and claim to “know" that embodiment, then you are both religious and pretentious.
