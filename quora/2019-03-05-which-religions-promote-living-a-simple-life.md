# Which religions promote living a simple life?

Buddhism, Christianity, Islam. I doubt there is a religion that does not. How the adherents of those religions struggle to complicate life is the true marvel. Such simplicity seems to not be natural to man.

It is inherent in us to be users of tools to make war with the earth and bend it to our fleeting will. The wise among us always realize that the earth abides. We do not. We would do better to lay down our tools and appreciate that which does not need to be hacked at and pontificated on.

I have been following your journey with admiration.
