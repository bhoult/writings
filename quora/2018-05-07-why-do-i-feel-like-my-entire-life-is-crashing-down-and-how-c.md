# Why do I feel like my entire life is crashing down and how can I fix it?

Think of your life as a pyramid. You are at the top. If the pyramid has a broad base and a narrow top it will be stable. If the earth moves it will remain upright.

If instead it is broad at the top balancing on a small point it will continually fall.

The top is composed of things you maintain. Your stuff that you buy and store, the car you make payments on, the apartment you pay rent for, the “friends” that constantly need something from you.

The bottom are things that support you without requiring maintenance. The house you paid off, the inexpensive car that is old but still works and requires no payments. Your true friends and family that you can depend on.

So, invest in expanding the base and reducing the peak. Move things from the top to the bottom. Pay off a debt, learn a skill, make an ally. Do not add to the top more than the base will support. Learn patience. Become more truthful with yourself about the actual cost of everything you add. It may not immediately seem like what you want, but if stability is your goal then it is what you need.
