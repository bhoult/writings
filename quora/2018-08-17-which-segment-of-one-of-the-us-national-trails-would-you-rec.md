# Which segment of one of the US National trails would you recommend to a reasonably fit middle aged woman, with two weeks to spend, hiking 5 miles per day, on average?

In Arkansas the buffalo River trail is probably my favorite in the spring or the fall. Winter is nice too if you are prepared for it. I would not suggest summer though, too many bugs, too hot.

It follows the bluffs along the buffalo River for about 60 miles, but if you start at the top is mostly downhill (with the river) and generally follows the contours of the land unlike the AT which wants you to go to the top and bottom of every single hill.

Since it follows the river you can always get easy access to water to drink or swim in. There are a ton of waterfalls, rock formations, historic homesteads civil war graveyards, caves, natural arches etc.

It is also not a heavily trafficked trail like the AT or PCT or JMT, so you won't constantly be running into other groups.
