# How can I land a web development job when they all seem to need 3+years of working experience?

You are in a room with one door. The door is locked, you don't have a key. What do you do?

Do you stand there looking at the door? Do you keep knocking and hoping someone will come? Do you play by the rules?

Or do you kick the door down, smash a hole in the wall, tear up the floorboards, light the ceiling on fire.

If they won't give you a job without experience then find your own jobs until you have the experience. Become self employed, find your own customers, make websites. Then in three years when they ask you to work for them you can tell them what you require.
