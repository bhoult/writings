# What do you do with 2 million dollars when you own your own home, no car payments, and you are 84 years old alone and in pretty good health?

I sometimes wonder what I would do in this situation. It is not likely I will have two million dollars, but I will probably have something that I can use. I think I would not want to do the “normal” thing, basically maximize my comfort until the end. I would probably look for something that would make those that knew me wonder what happened.

I think I would pretend I was poor and volunteer somewhere meaningful. I would look for opportunities to maximize the impact of whatever I had left because I am pretty sure none of it will be coming with me. I would find people with big dreams and small opportunities and I would help them to take a risk that they would not be able to afford otherwise.

I don’t expect this would be easy. Using up what you have left by giving it away would be easy… finding some charity to do it for you would be easy. Taking responsibility to find the best use of what you have before you go though, that should be hard and probably a little painful.

I personally hope to die with nothing when the time comes. To turn myself inside out and empty all that remains then travel lightly into whatever is next. I came with nothing… I want to go the same way.
