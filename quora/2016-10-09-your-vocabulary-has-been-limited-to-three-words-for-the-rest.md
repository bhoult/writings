# Your vocabulary has been limited to three words for the rest of your life. Which three do you choose?

I was thinking about how the modern graphical user interface is actually a regression from the communicative capabilities of the command line interface. Before the GUI you had to learn a collection of commands and you could string those commands together into sentences and tell the computer what you wanted.

But the world was filled with illiterates, so they reduced communication to pointing at pictures and clicking. If you visit a foreign country you will find yourself doing the same unless you know the language. The complexity of language is replaced with point and grunt.

So I would have two grunts Ugh and uuGh. And my third word would be “quiet!”, for when I was tired of all the small talk.
