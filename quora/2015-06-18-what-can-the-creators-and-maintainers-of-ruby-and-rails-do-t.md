# What can the Creators and maintainers of Ruby and Rails do to improve performance and scalability of the language and framework?

Well... to answer the question I suppose they could make a JIT compiled version with better multi-threading.

But it is kind of like asking why they don't make hammers that are better at spreading paint. It is not as if you are in a sealed box and someone said "Here is one tool... you must use if for everything, forever."

The creators of Ruby once wondered something like "Why does writing web applications in assembly suck so much. Maybe there is something we can do about that." I understand that it would be nice to have one best way to do everything and if you could just find that and master it then you would never have to learn anything else. 

The creators of ruby were not trying to create the one ring to rule them all. They wanted to make a tool to solve a specific set of problems well and they did that. Furthermore they made it open and encouraged it to play well with others who may do something else well.
