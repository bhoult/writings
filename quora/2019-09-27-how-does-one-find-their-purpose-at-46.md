# How does one find their purpose at 46?

I knew my purpose at 19, I just did not know the specifics and how to make it happen. I arranged my life accordingly and expected I would figure it out. By 30 the specifics were clear, but I cleverly made it dependent on the occurrence of outside events beyond my direct control. I just had to wait and be ready.

And nothing happened. So I built a theology of sorts to justify my unwillingness to take responsibility for my own situation. An extra insulating layer to shield my ego from my lack of initiative.

By 40 I began to suspect that I may not be the center of the universe. The rot had set in. My body was beginning to break down in mysterious and unamusing ways. I had been around long enough to notice my accomplishments fade, I began to forget them myself. I had to wonder why I had thought them so important once. This foolishness of scrawling our names on the water.

Now at 47 I can't tell you how to find your purpose. I do feel pretty confident that anyone who says they can is either a fool or a liar.

For a brief time, we are given to wandering the infinate. Not to capture or claim, but to witness and appreciate to the extent that our brief lives allow. We may stand together as on a mountain top and pointing into the distance tell the stories of where we have been. We may point to the future at adventures we hope to find. But there are no maps, the landscape eternally shifts below us.

The other option, the safe one, the one that will lead you quietly and comfortably to your grave is to expect your purpose to be handed to you. Stand refusing to move because the risk is too great. The infinite will resist being packaged, named, bounded, sold, defined, described, constrained, simplified, reduced. As you get older may you dissolve gracefully instead of making your world so small that you can claim purpose.
