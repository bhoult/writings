# How can I be a minimalist?

Not sure there is any trick to this... you just do it. Get rid of what you don't need and avoid getting more unless you really need it.

I am going to assume that you don't really know what you need, so if that is the problem then try this. Put everything you own into boxes, silverware, cups, clothes, books, tools, everything. Put them on one side of a room along with all your furniture.

Now if you need something go get it and put it in it's place. But first make sure that you can't do without or use something that is already out-of-the-box as a substitute. Like if you need a glass to drink out of, but there is a mug already out then just use the mug.

After a month or two the stuff left in the boxes will be stuff you either don't really need, or need infrequently. Go through it and pick out just a few things you really want or cant get rid of and put them in a trunk or closet, somewhere out of sight. Designate this space as your clutter space. The rule is that it if it fills up then you can't get more. Then rent a mini-storage or put everything else in a garage or somewhere it is not easy to get to. In a year or two, if it is still in the mini-storage then take it to salvation army or have a yard-sale.

Going forward, if you want something new then make yourself wait. My general rule is that I have to wait a week for every $100 the thing costs. Most clutter is impulse buys... avoid the impulse and you will avoid the buy. If you get something that is redundant (a better kitchen knife) then get rid of the old one.

You may find that you are paying for a lot of empty space at this point. So reduce the size of your home if possible. Make a tiny-house or rent a small apartment. Personally I built a 19ft geodesic dome out of conduit pipe and papercrete for $3000 and moved out into the woods. Your path will probably be different.

You may find that you are making more money than you need at this point. Maybe just work part time. Maybe get a job you enjoy more even though it pays less. 

You may find that you have more time. Now you have to figure out what to do with it. I suggest you find a nice tree to sit under on a sunny day and think about what you want out of the rest of your life. Maybe a project or goal you want to accomplish maybe you want to become more involved in your community, maybe you want to volunteer and help others, maybe you want to watch every program on Netflix. Keep sitting under that tree until you figure it out. It seemed to work for Buddha. 

You are now a "minimalist".
