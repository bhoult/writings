# If you could rid the English language of one word, which one would you remove?

First person pronouns. I, me, my etc.

Imagine sitting in a coffee shop filled with the usual hum of conversation. Somewhere a genie reluctantly emerges from his lamp and glares at the fool vigorously rubbing his domicile. The wish is uttered and suddenly nobody can refer to themselves ever again.

The endless stream of egotistical self centered revelation comes sputtering to an awkward halt. Panic ensues as nobody is able to continue blurting out their preferences, opinions, and achievements.

Someone painfully stumbles through an objective statement that communicates a concept in which they are not the center of the universe. Someone hears it and learns something new instead of merely waiting their turn to interject about themselves.

Teens everywhere go wide eyed in horror as they find themselves unable to do anything except grunt, and then grunt louder in an attempt to explain the previous grunt.

And everywhere noise is diminished as people cease their endless navel gazing to embark on a strange new era of communication.
