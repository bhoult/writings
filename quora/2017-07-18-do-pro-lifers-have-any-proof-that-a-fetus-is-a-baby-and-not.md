# Do pro-lifers have any proof that a fetus is a baby and not just a clump of cells, like an organ?

A fetus is a clump of cells that has a unique DNA that will become what you think of as a human. It has that potential.

When you were one year old child you were entirely dependent on your parents for survival, you were of no use to them and were probably pretty inconvenient. If that is all you would ever be then you would be little different than a cute tumor. But your parents envisioned you as becoming an independent human. You had that potential.

When you go to sleep at night and are unaware of your environment and unconscious of your joys and struggles it is not your past that gives your life value, but the expectation that you will wake up and live as a human. Even though you are of no immediate use to society you are still protected under the law because you have a future. One just has to wait a bit. You have that potential.

When you make a fetus it is a deliberate conscious act, it has consequences that are known. The act carries responsibilities that you are morally obligated to fulfill. If you are willing to take away the potential of this clump of cells that will be your child and make it pay for the choices you made then this to me is unjust.

Clint Eastwood's character in Unforgiven said something like this: “It is a hell of a thing to kill a man. You take away all that he has and all that he ever will have.” It is not the past or present of an individual that makes them a person. It is what they have the potential to be that you are responsible for if you choose to kill them.

And if you decide to kill them then live with that decision for what it is. Don’t dehumanize the action to trivialize it and make it appear less costly than it is. All the atrocities in history, slavery, genocides, holocausts, started with someone finding an excuse to make the person that is inconvenient to them less than human.
