# Is there a way to build a geodesic dome that does not leak?

Sure, basically make a geodesic dome that does not have seams. Or is covered with something without seams.

If you build a geodesic dome in the same way you build a square house, with rectangular timber and rectangular shingles on the roof it will likely leak because there are no square angles in the structure. So nothing will fit right, leaving cracks and gaps for water. Those that insist on using traditional materials can get kits that are precut by a computer so that the angles are all correct. But these kits are expensive.

If you use metal tubing for the structure then cover with a thick yurt like fabric, then the fabric should not leak for many years.

Monolithic domes are another option. These may not have the geodesic structure but they are the same shape in the end. You can make them by pouring cement over an inflated dome shape. Or out of cement blocks.

I have been living for the past 5 years in a geodesic dome made with steel conduit pipe. I covered it with shrink wrap plastic and sprayed papercrete onto that inside and outside. It is small, only 19ft in diameter but only cost $3000.

No leaks yet.
