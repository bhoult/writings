# Learning web development. Should I listen to people that says not to learn PHP at all?

You should not listen to anyone who says not to learn anything. When has anyone ever been harmed by learning something new, especially in programing where things are changing so rapidly. I personally dislike php, but the job of any programmer is to find the shortest path between the problem and its solution. If php is something you would have to go out of your way to go around on that path just to avoid a small investment in learning time then reaching the goal is not your priority. All learning is good, even if just to know what and why other languages may be better suited to a task than php.
