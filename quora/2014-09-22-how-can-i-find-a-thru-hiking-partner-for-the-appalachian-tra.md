# How can I find a thru-hiking partner for the Appalachian Trail?

I did the whole thing in 2004 and agree with what Greg said. The problem is that people hike at different speeds and it is frustrating trying to stay with someone whose speed differs from yours by too much. If they are faster than you then you will probably push too hard risking injury. If they are slower then you will resent not making your distance goals because you have to wait for them.

The thing is that there are probably going to be thousands of people at the beginning if you are northbound. You will hang out with them at shelters and after a few days you will notice the people that seem to end up where you are each night. You will probably end up hiking with them more over the next few weeks, and then you will have a group of 7-10 "hiking partners". I am still friends with the people I met 10 years later.

Don't try to form preconceived notions of how it will be and then try to make "plans". It will be like nothing you have ever experienced before so just be ready to adapt and enjoy the journey.
