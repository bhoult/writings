# How can one follow a minimalist and frugal lifestyle in London?

I keep seeing variations on this question on Quora. In your case being in London seems like it should differentiate the problem somehow. I am not sure how, you should be able to be who you want to be wherever you are.

I guess I am starting to wonder whether the questioner(s) are really wanting to achieve what they ask about or if they have decided that it may be a good look for them to try out. Like living frugally may have become fashionable at some point to the great amusement of everyone living in poverty. I don't know you, so maybe I am overgeneralizing, but to live with less is not like something you have to have a plan to accomplish. You just do it. Get rid of what you don't need. Don't buy stuff you can do without.

I am going to assume that you have an ideal minimalist vision of yourself. Something clean, uncluttered, efficient and free. I imagine that you think that if you could be this vision that your friends would respect and admire you. Maybe you are just tired of dealing with the clutter and junk all around you. Maybe you want to save money, have more time, more freedom, whatever. 

I would advise that you focus on this, what you think minimalism will accomplish. If you can't get rid of what you don't want then you need to figure out the reason for that. Because if you have not done it yet then you really don't believe it. You are asking for clever techniques to test something without risking anything if it turns out to be wrong.

If you really did not want the stuff then getting rid of it would not be a problem. Even in London. Therefore, figure out what it is the stuff does for you that you are afraid to be without. Then think about why you believe that, what made you that way. Many of our lifestyle choices were made without thought in our foolish youth. Revisit those assumptions, change who you really are rather than who you appear to be and your lifestyle will naturally change to reflect that.
