# Being a web developer in the freelance market how much useful it would be to learn graphic design?

Very useful. I have been a freelance web developer for over 20 years. Technically I have no trouble, but artistically I am lacking.

This leads to additional iterations of back and forth with the customer before they are satisfied.

To me the website is functional, easy to read and navigate and looks clean. Job done. To them it does not look quite like what they had in mind, they are not sure why but they don't like it. Something needs to be done.

Endless frustration.
