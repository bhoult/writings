# What might motivate someone to hike the entire Appalachian trail?

I did a through hike in 2004. I have been asked this question a number of times since then. It seems odd to me how my own answers vary so much. I think it comes down to it is not what you are currently tired of doing… of being.

My days all blend together, work, sleep, maybe do something on the weekend… then repeat. For days, months, years. I live the same day over and over. It is not bad enough to do anything about, it is not good enough to be satisfied with.

I see a dog spinning in circles, chasing its tail around and around. I laugh at the foolish dog and then go on through the same motions I have performed a thousand times. A lifetime of circles leading nowhere.

So why do something like the AT? It is not productive, you don't get a prize. It is the hardest work you will ever do, and you will have to pay to do it.

It is because it is so not whatever you are currently doing over and over. It is about as far from routine life that you can get. It looks nothing like a circle that you can chase your tail around. For a while you can go in a straight line all the way from Georgia to Maine.
