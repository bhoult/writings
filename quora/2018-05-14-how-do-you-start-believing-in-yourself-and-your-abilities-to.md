# How do you start believing in yourself and your abilities to to take on big life changing goals?

I think you have the order wrong. You don't believe in yourself and then take on big goals. You first merely find the courage to take on big goals even though you expect to fail.

Are you willing to fail? Think about pride and fear. How they end your journey before you even try. These are your enemies. They must be sacrificed.

Then you find the humility to see the situation as it really is and honesty to assess who you really are. Then you learn how to do better and find the determination to get up and try again and the persistence to keep on failing until you have gained the skill necessary to succeed.

You have to learn to be patient with yourself, which will lead you to be empathetic with the struggles of others. This will allow you to find allies who can help you learn that your goal was probably too small.

When you have found courage, humility, honesty, determination, persistence, patience, and empathy then you will have a reason to believe in yourself. Others will have a reason to believe in you. And your goal will have been already achieved.
