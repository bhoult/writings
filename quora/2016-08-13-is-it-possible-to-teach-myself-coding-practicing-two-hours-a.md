# Is it possible to teach myself coding, practicing two hours a day and earn $2000 a month from home in a year or less?

There are really two skills you will need to accomplish this and they are just about polar opposites in terms of required mindset/ personality. The first part... Programming, requires extended concentration, specific unambiguous goals, appeals to introverts. The second part "make $2000 a month" requires sales. Sales works best with vague descriptions and often empty promises. You want to get the most you can from the customer for the least effort. You tell them what they want to hear in order to close the deal. It requires that you be adept at people manipulation which is the domain or extroverts.

There are very few people with this magic combination.

If you don't totally suck at sales though then you might consider web development instead of programming to start with. Using a CMS like WordPress, joomla or drupal along with prepackaged themes, you could become fairly proficient in the timeframe you mentioned and could start to make money. A few basic websites for local small businesses should earn you about $2000.

While you can build CMS websites without programming, learning JavaScript or a server side language will increase the capability and appeal of your websites. You will eventually learn programming as a side effect while being paid to do it. Then if programming is what you want to do, you will have experience to put on your resume and a portfolio of work to show them. This will go a lot further than telling them you spent two hours a day studying for a year.
