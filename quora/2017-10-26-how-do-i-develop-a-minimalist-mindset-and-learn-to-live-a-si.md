# How do I develop a minimalist mindset and learn to live a simple life?

It’s a good question, for which I wish there were an easy answer. If there were I imagine minimalism would be more approachable.

Of the answers here I think Kalani Tom’s comes closest. What you are asking is basically how to like something that you don’t really like, don’t really feel is true. I don’t believe you can yet. Minimalism is not a pit you force yourself to climb into for your own good, it is the path of escape when you perceive that you have been in the pit all along.

So ask yourself if you are free and don’t make it into a binary answer. You are more free than many, less free than you could be. What holds you back, keeps you bound.

Think about what you want from life, where your ideal life would be, what it would look like, then study what stands in the way between who you are now and who you want to be. How free do you need to be to become who you want to become?

The possessions you have are meant to help you get there, but often they are among the very things that hold you back. Identify those that keep you from your goal either because you have to maintain them, or store them, or guard them, or make payments on them.

It is a hard choice to change your life, there will come a point when you will have to take steps that frighten you. Make sure you do not sabotage your own goals by finding ways to fail. Think of all the times you said or thought “I would but…”, and then returned to your comfortable routine.

Possessions, purchases, consumerism, provide a temporary sense of accomplishment. It is a brief respite from the sense that you have failed to achieve your true goals. A distraction that makes the failure still harder to overcome. If it becomes hard enough, maybe you will be able to give up and forget about it. Just be satisfied with how things are. If you can make it too hard to succeed then it is easier to accept that you failed.

If you know where you want to go, who you want to be and have found the courage and determination to proceed then minimalism is just a way to lighten your load. When you truly want your freedom it will not be hard.
