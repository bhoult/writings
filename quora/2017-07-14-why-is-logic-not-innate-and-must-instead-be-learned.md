# Why is logic not innate and must, instead, be learned?

Because logical thinking does not give you a reason to live. It helps you to overcome obstacles, it tells you what you should avoid to not get in trouble, it makes you powerful but it does not make you joyful.

Look at a sunset, logic will tell you it is just light refracting off different molecules and densities in the atmosphere.

Ask why you love, logic will tell you it is endorphins, and chemicals interacting with electrical signals in your brain.

Ask why you live, logic will tell you that you might have 50 years left if you are lucky and that all you are and accomplish during that span will be meaningless in the billions of years to come.

Ask your worth, logic will tell you that you are one of billions acting as a parasite on the planet… just a collection of temporarily sentient dirt soon to return from whence you came.

Be careful how you idolize logic, it takes as much as it gives. This is a trade that should be made with understanding. I am grateful we start with joy and mystery and wonder.
