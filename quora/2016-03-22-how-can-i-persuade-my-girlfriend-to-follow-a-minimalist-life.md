# How can I persuade my girlfriend to follow a minimalist lifestyle? (considering I'm a minimalist)

I don't think you can. Furthermore by pressuring her she will likely become resistant. When it comes down to it people have to make up their own mind on an issue and if you respect them then you have to respect their decision.

I believe you can only do the following two things.

Let her know why minimalism is important to you. If she values your opinion then she will want to know about this aspect of your life. Let her know where this idea came from, what it has accomplished, what you hope that it will accomplish in the future and why. Be transparent but not argumentative. Make her aware of it as an option, not something you want to force her into. Provide an example of the benefits. If you are doing it right, if your philosophy has merit, then there should be tangible benefits. If she can see this and admires it then she will want to emulate your example. If she cannot see the benefit then why should she be persuaded. In other words, be the best mimimalist you can be... this is your wordless argument.It is important that this be her choice because it can be a hard choice in the short term. It will require sacrifice of the immediate in order to attain long term benefits. If she does it just to make you happy, then she will see only the sacrifice and will grow to resent it and you by association. If you pressure her then she will likely start lying to you so as not to upset you or cause an argument. Don't go down this road.

In the end minimalism is a form of asceticism as opposed to consumerism which is a form of hedonism. It is living deliberately against impulse. Those that follow it usually have cause to regret following past impulses without consideration. Without the experience of either learning from your own mistakes or the insight to learn from the mistakes of others you are unlikely to follow a path which promotes deprivation. You have likely had your epiphany because of your experience. She has not. Share your experience and she may be able to see it without having to go through it herself.
