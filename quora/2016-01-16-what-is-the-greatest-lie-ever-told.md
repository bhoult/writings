# What is the greatest lie ever told?

We carry within our skull a few pounds of fat containing a mass of neurons. We expose this lump of flesh to the reflected light of a miniscule portion of the universe over the brief span of a life. We are revealed, naked, small and vulnerable. 

We are ashamed, afraid. We struggle to ignore our ignorance, we find faith, we declare that we "know" something... That we know anything. We deny the faith that brought us here and are transformed into arrogant fools believing ourselves suddenly wise. This is our lie. That the finite has mastered the infinite.

We gather into groups based on our shared lies. Gray areas are confusing and uncomfortable, we polarize. Beliefs become facts. Facts become truth. Truth can predict the future. The future that is dark and full of monsters. Those who hold differing truths shake our foundations. They threaten the future we presume to know. We hate them. We separate, we fight, we kill. If we eradicate them our truth becomes enshrined in culture. Our culture is superior...based on our finite truth, it is beyond question. It is known. Contradictions are malicious lies, they threaten our future. We war.

But through it all new minds continue to be exposed to infinite light and old minds take their insecurities to the grave. Perhaps our children will learn to be more humble and more wise.
