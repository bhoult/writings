# I've got a banner image on the homepage. In the lower part of the banner I want to reduce the opacity. Is there a way to do this using css?

As far as I know, you cannot do a transparency gradient on an image in CSS. You can do it in a PNG image though, so convert your banner to a PNG and add an opacity mask to that using gimp or Photoshop.
