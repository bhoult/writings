# I want to start an organization that builds geodesic domes for the poor as homes. How do I do it?

I have been living for three years in a geodesic dome I built myself out of metal conduit pipe, shrink wrap and papercrete for about $3000. I think I could get it below $1500 if I were doing it again. I blogged about the whole process. Go to http://Minimalintentions.com and click on dome at the top.
