# Why do people like minimalism? It means nothing but less and restricted.

A harbor freight store recently opened in my hometown. As I wandered around I had a kind of fantasy of how potentially powerful I could be if only I had the right tool. For $800 I could have a metal lathe, imagine all the things I could do with that. For $100 I could get this fancy saw… I have one already but this one has a bigger blade and other cool features.

In my mind I saw myself fabricating objects, constructing buildings. The fantasy is that the right stuff will make this all possible, suddenly it will be easy enough that I am motivated to do something useful instead of watching Netflix.

I fell for this nonsense enough to realize that buying stuff to empower myself for potential productivity merely leads to a lack of money and a pile of useless junk. If there is a will then a rock and a stick would probably suffice.

Minimalism is not being tricked into trading your actual life for the fantasy of one.
