# How do I make an interior design model that can be imported into Unity?

Not sure why you are asking me this. I have never used unity. But if it were me I would use blender, it is free and has a much better rendering engine than sketchup and although I have not done it personally I know it can export to unity. 

Blender can also make animated walk through videos and has a "game engine" built in if you need interactivity.
