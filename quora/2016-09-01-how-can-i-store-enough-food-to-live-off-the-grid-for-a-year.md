# How can I store enough food to live off the grid for a year cheaply?

Beans, rice, salt pepper, sugar, oil in plastic 5 gallon buckets. You should be able to buy in bulk for a few hundred dollars.

You may want to mix that up a bit with other bulk grains like wheat, oats or dried corn.

I would also get a book on foraging since you will want to supplement that for the sake of flavor, variety and vitamins.

If you plan on storing it for a while, get some of those chemical hand warmers that get hot when you open the bag and throw one in each bucket. They will remove any oxygen or humidity in the bucket. Seal the bucket with hot wax or silicon caulking.
