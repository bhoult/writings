# Why do hikers go off hiking and then inconvenience everyone around them because they get lost?

The same reason drivers go places and inconvenience everyone when they break down. Or people choose to live in houses even though it annoys others when they catch on fire.

Oh but we're we like the Noble crustacean, never attempting anything where we might fail. Safely quivering in our dark hole.
