# Why is it better to sleep without clothes in a sleeping bag when camping in a cold area?

Because mummy bags are very restrictive of movement. It is somewhere between sleeping on your bed and sleeping in a straight jacket. Clothes don't move around well in a sleeping bag, so if you move a lot in your sleep you will end up turning yourself into an uncomfortable knot in the night at which point you will wake up and have to sort yourself out.

I have heard it said that it helps the bag distribute body heat better. This has not been my experience. Basically if you have a good zero degree bag in 30 degree weather you will be too hot and having less clothes would help you vent heat better.

If you are at the limit of your bags rating or if it is an old bag that has started clumping then there will be cold spots so you will have to wear clothing to compensate… regardless of what kind of tangled mess you end up in during the night.
