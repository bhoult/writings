# How can I live young, wild, and free?

Young is the easy one. Start immediately, you will never be any younger.

Wild implies that you live without using that big brain you are burdened with. Like a wild animal. Follow any emotion or impulse you may have. Be careful not to think about it or it's consequences.

Free means that you have to avoid any cost or consequence. Not just financial. Every action has consequences, sometimes small, sometimes life altering… but don't think about that too much. Concentrate on telling convincing lies and shifting blame elsewhere. Study narcissists and sociopaths, they have this all figured out.

And there you go… young, wild and free. The world is yours to burn.
