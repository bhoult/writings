# What are some hacks for living in a small space?

There are a couple of strategies that you can follow, either make creative ways to store more stuff in a small space, or get less stuff to store.

As far as finding more places to store space, consider that insulation is basically a air gap where the air does not move much in order to reduce the conduction and convection of heat. This in normally done with some kind of insulation (fiberglass, foam, etc.). You might consider using less of the traditional insulation and then making small storage spaces covering the entirety of the walls, floors, ceilings. Line them with shredded cloth (or pillow stuffing) to keep it snug then store stuff in these spaces. That way they do double duty as insulation + storage.

Getting less stuff to store is more in line with minimalist ideals. Work at getting rid of things you don't constantly use and try to find multiple uses for what you decide to keep. For example, I recently got a Vitamix that replaces my old blender, but also replaces the food processor, coffee grinder, and cheese grater and a multitude of other small tools. Finding appliances that serve multiple purposes and using them for every possible use is key. Then get rid of redundancy.

Start with the least that you feel is practical, then periodically review what is there and try to see if you could get rid of something else.
