# Why is survival considered a necessity? What is the purpose of survival if you have no interest in the future?

Because it is a prerequisite to literally everything else. If you have no interest in yourself or anyone else or anything else then at least consider that things change. You are not the same now as you once were. You will be someone in the future that you aren't now. If you survive. If not then this is as good as it gets.
