# How possible is it to find a professional coder with Android experience who I can partner or work with?

Should be pretty easy if you frame the request fairly. Something like this: I have a number of great app ideas, I would like to partner with a programmer as I have little programming experience. For my part I will work full time and will give you half my income allowing you to work full time on our apps. We will split the ownership and profit evenly between us.

Most programmers have dozens of "good ideas" already. What they lack is time to work on them because all their productive time is spent making a living. Your idea has little value, your initiative does.
