# If you work out like crazy, can you eat a lot and not get fat?

Go hike the Appalachian trail. It is like a 2000 mile all you can eat buffet. Every few days when you reach a town you can eat all you want. I lost 80 lbs and ate everything that would fit in my mouth over a 6 month period.
