# Is it legal to use another website image using HTML code <img src ="another_website_image.jpg"> without asking permission to the author?

Unless you have permission from the creator / owner of the image it is illegal to use it in both cases unless they explicitly release it to the public domain or put it under something like the creative commons licence. You may assert that “lots of people do this”, which is true and you may get away with it if the owner does not care. But if they do and they find out then you can be sued.

What you are specifically asking about is called “hot linking” and is considered impolite. Essentially, along with stealing someone else’s work, you are now stealing the hosting that they are paying for as well. Again, you can probably get away with it for a while… just as someone could probably get away with stealing the change out of your home for a while.

Another thing to consider though is that you are not actually specifying the image in the url… you are just specifying the location of (a) image. That image can be changed at any time. Sometimes when people discover people hot linking their images they replace them with something else just to get the hot-linker in trouble. Sometimes they just block your ip so all your images break.

To summarize: Stealing is bad. Inconvenience is not an excuse to steal. Don’t be bad.
