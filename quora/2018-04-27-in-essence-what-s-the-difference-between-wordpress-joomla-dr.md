# In essence, what's the difference between WordPress, Joomla & Drupal?

Basically they all accomplish the same thing in the end.

Drupal is probably the most flexible in that you can define your own data structures and relationships beyond the standard articles, users etc. But this flexibility comes at the cost of complexity and speed. If you want to do something non standard and don't want to custom program it then Drupal may work.

Joomla approaches content management like a newspaper would. You have content editors, publishers etc. Articles are organized by menus, categories etc. It is not as flexable as drupal, but is easier to use for its intended purpose. If you are making a corporate or institutional site that will be managed by multiple people in different roles then Joomla is probably what you want.

WordPress evolved from a blogging platform and is by far the most popular. It is best suited for sites managed by an individual. It is the simplest to learn and use. However its popularity makes it the prime Target for hackers so make backups and avoid non mainstream plugins.

They all have a huge ecosystem of themes and plugins that expand their functionality into other areas like shopping carts or image galleries. By using these any of these systems can extended to be functionally equivalent to any other.

Basically, if it is weird then use Drupal or custom program it. If you are working for an institution with multiple roles publishing newspaper like content or a corporate website then use Joomla. Otherwise use WordPress.
