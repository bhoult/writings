# What is the most minimalistic way to live one's life?

Daydreaming.

Sit amidst your current environment and imagine how it could be better. Ponder that bookcase in the corner filled with various stuff. Could you do without some or most of it? Maybe you could get rid of the whole bookcase. Then there would be an open space. Less to clean, less to move around.

How about all those spices that expired years ago? Will you ever really use them? Imagine what your kitchen would look like completely empty, then add just the things you want.

I would not do anything at all until you have a clear vision of what you want. Maybe just do one room at a time. But the first step is mental, make a cup of tea, sit in the room, look around and imagine.
