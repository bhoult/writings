# How many people are you likely to see on the Appalachian Trail in the first days and weeks?

There are about 3000 that start each year. Most within a couple months March through april. In the first couple weeks he will probably meet 50 to 100 people each day. It can get crowded at shelters. These people are invariably helpful and excited about what they are trying to do. They will range in age from high school kids to 70+ years old. They will be doctors, lawyers, students and unemployed homeless people. They will take new name so they can walk as equals.

About half will drop out in the first month. By then your son will probably have found a group that goes about his speed. They will sometimes walk together but will often just meet up at the end of the day. He will cross paths with plenty of people during the day. Other through hikers just a little faster or slower, day hikers, trail maintainers etc. We used to joke that if you are alone and need somebody just try to pee behind a tree and someone will inevitably show up.

By the end he will have a close group of friends that he will probably stay in touch with for the rest of his life. The group will be smaller, but they will all be experts by this point. He will not be the same person when he returns. And only him and the people he meets will understand why.
