# What does minimalist mean?

Minimal simply means less or least. Minimalism is a philosophy or ideal or artistic expression which attempts to accomplish its goal using the least resources. As a philosophy or lifestyle it attempts to minimize the quantity of material possessions and other nonessentials in order to maximize other aspects such as time, experience or freedom. Quality of materials or experience is deliberately maximized over quantity. Clutter is to be avoided.

It is in most ways the opposite of consumerism. A consumer typically looks for a product to solve any given problem and will try to collect as many products as possible over a lifetime so as to be prepared for any future contingencies. These products have to be maintained, stored and organized in order to be effective. This requires a certain expenditure of an individuals life, will tie them to the location it is stored and will encumber their mobility and adaptability.

For these reasons a minimalist would choose to avoid collecting possessions unless absolutely necessary. If the need was temporary they would borrow or rent something. If it was an recurring need they would get only what was necessary and would probably try to maximize quality and durability. For example a consumer generally has a kitchen full of cheap dull knives. A minimalist would only have a few and they would be sharp and high quality.
