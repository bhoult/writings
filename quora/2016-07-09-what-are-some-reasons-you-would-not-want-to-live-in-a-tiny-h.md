# What are some reasons you would not want to live in a Tiny House?

I live in a tiny house, a 19ft diameter dome. I would not want a larger house... more to deal with, more to maintain, more to clean.

I suspect you are trying to persuade someone else to live in a tiny house and hope to anticipate their objections. So perhaps you like me also live in a tiny house. So ask yourself what disadvantages there would be to living in a still tinier house. Would you be comfortable halving the size of what you currently consider comfortable? I read that itinerant railroad workers used to store and transport their tools in a coffin shaped box. While working the box doubled as their home. Tiny is a matter of perspective.

If the prospect of living in a coffin seems unpleasant to you then it will be for similar reasons that living in a tiny house may seem unpleasant to others.

From a practical perspective for me the biggest disadvantage is that half my tiny house is tool storage. I also try to be a minimalist but if you work on physical things then this is often accomplished with a multitude of tools which require storage, which requires space. It is not possible for me to just work for an hour or so on some project since half that time would be spent unpacking the required tools and the other half would be putting them away. So my goal is now tiny house + a separate cluttered workshop. These are idealisticly opposites, which will be frustrating. However, just as one should use the best tool for the job, I am learning that one should choose the best environment for the lifestyle they wish to live.
