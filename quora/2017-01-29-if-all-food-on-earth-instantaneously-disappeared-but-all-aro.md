# If all food on Earth instantaneously disappeared but all around you for miles were humans lying in permanent comas, would you eat them to stay alive?

What a strange world that would be. Any animal could be used as food, so all animals have disappeared. Insects likewise are gone. Grass seeds, pine trees all fruits and nuts also gone. Seaweed and sea life also disappears. Without pollinators almost everything else would be dead soon after.

So your world that remains is one of minerals, deadly chemicals, sterile waters. Plantless and lifeless and inexplicably littered with 7 billion people lying in some kind of coma. They would all die after a couple days without water. Leaving you pondering whether or not to start eating one of them to extend your life a few days longer before they all become inedible from decomposition… assuming that ecoli is considered inedible.

So, no … I would find some barren rocky precipice to perch on and calmly watch the sun rise and set until the light goes out for me also.
