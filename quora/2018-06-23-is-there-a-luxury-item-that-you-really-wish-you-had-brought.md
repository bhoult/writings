# Is there a luxury item that you really wish you had brought on your thru-hike?

A wallet full of cash would have been nice. Good quality lightweight gear is a bit of a luxury, get as much of that as you can afford. Freeze dried food like Mountain House is light, easy to prepare fairly tasty but expensive. I would have taken more of it if I could afford it.

Really, the question I was constantly asking myself was what could I get rid of so I did not have to haul it around.

Perhaps a sherpa carrying a lazyboy recliner would have been a good luxury item.
