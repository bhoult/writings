# How has moving to a smaller house and minimizing your things been helpful?

Well, the obvious change is financial, buying less stuff because there is no place to put it. Less to heat and cool. Also more free time with less to maintain and clean. I go out more, the world doubles as my living room. I spend a lot of time at coffee shops people watching and reading. I am naturally non-social so this encourages me to not become a complete hermit.

It seems like people try to create their own artificial environment so they can isolate themselves from a world where they lack control. Having a minimal house encourages me to live in the real world, where I am just a small fish in a vast pond. I am insignificant, weak and vulnerable but I can still see the stars. I can retreat to my small home, but there is nothing there for me but a temporary refuge.

The benefit is not direct. It is not like living in a small space will immediately resolve some problem or make me a better person. It is more like a first step. Like a recovering alcoholic resolving not to go into a bar. I am a consumer dissatisfied with that role, minimalism is part of my rebellion.
