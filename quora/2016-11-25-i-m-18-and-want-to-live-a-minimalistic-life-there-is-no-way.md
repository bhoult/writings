# I'm 18 and want to live a minimalistic life, there is no way I can make my parents cooperate. How should I start?

What kind of cooperation do you need from your parents? Minimalism is a personal decision, it does not require anything on their part, are you trying to get them to participate?

If you are not trying to change their behavior then for yourself you just start being what you want to be. Maybe give away all your junk to charity. If your parents want to punish you for that then suggest they confiscate more of your stuff.

Attachment to things is one of the means that society threatens us into conformity. Without attachments there is nothing to threaten to take away, nothing to grab hold of and pull you around. You are free. If they find a way to still manipulate you then thank them for helping you identify it and continue on.

There is no conflict with your parents, they are your environment and you are free to move within that. It is only when you both want to dominate some part that you come into conflict. It they demand control, then freely give it to them. As a minimalist you are not encumbered by these things.
