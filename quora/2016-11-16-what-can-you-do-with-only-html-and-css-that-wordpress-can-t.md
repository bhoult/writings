# What can you do with only HTML and CSS that WordPress can't do?

As others have mentioned your question as stated is flawed. I am going to guess you probably have “made a website” with wordpress and you are now wondering why everyone is saying you need to know html and CSS. After all, you did just fine without knowing any of that.

The answer is that you did not make a website. You just picked a premade one then added some content text using the tools provided. If you go buy a car and specify that you want it in black and you want leather seats you do not then tell people that you made your car.

In wordpress you “picked” your website. If you modified the CSS/htm then you “customized” your website. If you create your own template using html/CSS then you “made” your website.

In a practical sense if you don't know html/CSS then you can't make anything you want. If you have a clear vision of how you want it to be then you are limited to just finding something as close as possible. You are basically a consumer wandering through Walmart looking for a generic print of a painting to hang on your wall. Finding one does not make you an artist. Learning html and CSS then applying it is how you “make” a website.
