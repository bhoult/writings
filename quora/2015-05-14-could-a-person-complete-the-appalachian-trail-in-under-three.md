# Could a person complete the Appalachian Trail in under three months with two years of training? If so, what would that training look like?

It is certainly doable... I did it in seven months but wish I could have taken longer. In the process I learned to some extent that it is the journey that is satisfying. The end is just the end. 

At the rate you propose you will probably need a support team. You will have to go extremely light or risk injury. You will see some beautiful country but will have to admire it while walking as you will not have time to stop. If three month is all you have then consider doing it in pieces over several years and give yourself the chance to enjoy it.

To train I would suggest hiking the Appalachian trail. If that is not logistically feasible then get as close as you can. Weekend hikes up and down as many hills as possible carrying as heavy a load as you can. Lose as much weight as possible, there is not much difference between being 20lbs overweight and carrying a 20lb backpack.

Good luck. I am guessing you are young and looking for an accomplishment that others will admire. I know I was to some extent. I will warn you that nobody will admire you though. They will not even be able to really comprehend what you did and in so much as they do they will generally not care. It is too far outside their reality and as such it will just be an abstract curiosity at best.

If you do it for others, you will be disappointed. If you do it for yourself it will be wonderful and you will wish it could go on forever.
