# Is it possible or desirable to have too much freedom?

If you are omnipotent then it is not possible to have too much freedom. For the rest of us, although we don't like being told we are acting like an idiot or doing something wrong, we should be thankful when we are prevented from proving it.

You might ask if it is possible for a small child to have too much freedom... like freedom to run in the road, drink bleach, throw knives at his friends, etc. Most would agree, that would be too much freedom.

The problem comes when our freedom is restricted by someone we view as not having the authority, or wisdom to do so. Then I suppose the question becomes "Is it possible to have too much pride?"
