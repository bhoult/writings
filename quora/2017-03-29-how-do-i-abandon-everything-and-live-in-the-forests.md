# How do I abandon everything and live in the forests?

Once again it appears that Quorans would rather tell you how bad an idea this is than answer the actual question. It is human nature to assume anyone that wants to step outside the normal expectations must be wrong and in need of correction. Their arguments basically boil down to “This is unknown to me, I am afraid of the unknown, so don’t do it.”

By now you presumably understand the dangers. Consider though that many generations of your ancestors were apparently able to manage this impossible feat and that there are still people throughout the world living as you describe. “Modern” people look back on these people and assume that their lives were brutal, painful and short. This justifies their decision to be part of a modern society where they trade a portion of their life and independence for an assumption of security and protection.

What you lack that our ancestors had was knowledge and experience. You have been living in an artificial bubble that encourages more abstract life skills. You propose leaving that bubble and will need new more practical skills to be effective.

How you do this is slowly, and cautiously. Start by selling everything you can and living off as little as possible. In other words wean yourself off society and establish a safety net. When you leave society it will take some practice, so when you fail you should be able to return, regroup and try again. Society lives off cash, so have some in reserve while you learn. It is tempting to just throw caution to the wind and go all in. Being unprepared is a recipe for failure, you will probably survive but will end up back in society afraid to try again, with everyone pointing out how foolish you were.

During this time educate yourself with the resources society provides. Since you are not going to be watching TV spend the time learning about edible plants and basic survival and medical skills from books. Go on camping trips, take as little as possible. You will need to understand how hard it is to dig holes, cut trees, move rocks, plant food, hunt, trap, fish, be hungry, cold, hot, exhaust yourself. You can’t understand these things without doing them. You can’t plan how to live until you understand.

Go on a long hike with others, like the Appalachian Trail, or the Pacific Crest Trail. This will give you experience, strength and endurance. It will teach you to live with just what you can carry on your back. It will take you about six months to complete… during this time you will be half way between modern society and your goal. When you complete this you will know if you want to continue.

Finally sell the remainder of your possessions, pick a spot and begin.
