# What are your favorite backpacking hacks or tricks?

Pace your breathing and heart rate, not your speed. Pacing in hiking….and I am finding in life in general is the key to reaching your goal. You need to find the boundary between challenging and miserable and stay as close to it as possible for as long as long as you can.

When hiking find the rate you can go at which you are breathing hard, deep (but comfortable) breaths and modify your pace to keep your breathing constant. Do this by changing the size of your steps not the rate. Try to keep the rate constant.

When you start going uphill take small steps. Over time your body will adapt and your speed will become more regular. If you have to stop to catch your breath then either your stride was too long or the rate of your breathing was too fast.

Slow down… pace yourself.
