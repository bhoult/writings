# What shall I do when parents are too religious and force you to fulfill their useless dreams when you are completely financially dependent on them and they totally refuse to listen to you?

Start with a more accurate assessment of the situation. Rarely is the reality of a thing 100% true or false, although it is easier to think of it as though it were. Polarizing your position in this way merely justifies your belief that you are being wronged and puts you in the position where you are powerless. It is likely the exact same mode of thought that your parents are exhibiting which is causing your frustration.

Dogmatism can come in many forms.

What is “Too religious”? How much of the religion do you believe in? How much can you respect? How much can you accept? How much are you willing to endure? How much must you completely oppose?

Which dream(s) are useless? Are some of them useful? Can you cooperate with the ones that you see that have potential? Can you rationally explain your objections to the ones that don't? How are they “forcing” you? Pressuring is not the same as forcing.

Are you completely financially dependent? Could you get a job? Could you make a deal to do chores in exchange for not being forced to participate in the things you most object to? Are they threatening to withhold food and shelter if you don’t participate? These are the only things they are obligated to provide (assuming you are underage).

Do they “totally” refuse to listen to you? Are you saying something more persuasive than this question, or just complaining, perhaps stomping your feet and yelling a little before stomping off? Communication goes both ways, what you describe is merely two sides making assertions. Do you fully understand their position and the reasoning behind it? If not ask … and really listen, don’t object… in fact, take notes. Then do some research on why you disagree, and come back and present your views.

Do not become angry, that is weakness. Do not complain, yell or sulk, that demonstrates you are powerless. Do not behave in such a way as to justify the limitations they place on you, that is defeat.

Compromise… know where you are willing to bend and where to hold strong. Not every irritant has equal worth.

If you are not better at this than your parents then you do not deserve to be in control.
