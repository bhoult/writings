# What are some tips or guidelines a freelance designer can follow to manage a difficult, over-communicative client?

Bill them for it. On the invoice mark it as additional discovery research, or customer training, or additional customer documentation, or a change order on the original project specifications.

I only do this to particularly annoying customers, but have found it pretty effective. If they object then agree to remove the charge but explain that answering their questions takes the same amount of time as writing their code and you bill based on the hours you work.
