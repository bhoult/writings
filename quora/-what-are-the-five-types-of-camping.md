# What are the five types of camping?

According to the dept. of neolithic standards they are as follows:

Vehicle camping
Tent camping
Passed out drunk on the lawn camping
Camping inside of a hollowed out mastadon or other critter. “Carcass camping”
Finally, “Dirt camping” in which you sleep in a box buried in the ground for extended periods.
