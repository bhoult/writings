# How much does a freelance web design work cost?

Don’t you love it when nobody will give a straight answer? Of course it is true that it varies from place to place, programmer to programmer, but to answer your question…

As a “full stack” rails developer in rural Arkansas USA, I charge $50/hr. If I spend a lot of time figuring something out I don’t usually bill for that time since I consider that at $50 I should mostly know what I am doing.

And I drive used cars that average about $2000 to buy. Of course as others have mentioned you can spend more or less than that.
