# How much would it cost to build a hemispherical underground monolithic dome home with a radius of 30 feet?

It depends on how you would go about building it. If I were doing it I would use cement blocks. Basically put an anchor in the middle and attach a rod on a pivot. Use that to position the blocks at the correct angle and just work your way around mortaring them in place. Then build a rebar lattice around the outside. Add dirt in layers and use it as a form to pour cement around the rebar. Until it is buried to where you want.

Calculate the surface area, you want, divide by the size of a cement block, then add the cost of whatever thickness of poured cement you want and the cost of rebar.
