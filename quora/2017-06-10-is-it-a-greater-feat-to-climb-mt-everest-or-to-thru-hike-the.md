# Is it a greater feat to climb Mt. Everest or to thru-hike the Appalachian Trail?

There are very few flat parts on the AT. If you add up all of the elevation change it is equivalent to climbing Everest 16 times. I completed an AT through hike, but have never done Everest.

In terms of an accomplishment though I would rate Everest higher. The AT is definitely hard, but it is mostly an exercise in perseverance. If you are careful and don't give up anyone can complete it.

My impression of Everest is that the unprepared will probably fail, and could very likely die. If you say that Everest requires sustaining a level 10 effort for a for a month or two, then the AT is perhaps like sustaining a level 6 effort for six months.
