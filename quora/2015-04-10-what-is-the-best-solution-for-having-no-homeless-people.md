# What is the best solution for having no homeless people?

I am not sure "creative" solutions are the best approach. Perhaps deliberate, methodical, practical and persistent solutions would help more. People tend toward "creative" because it focuses more on their solution, than the unsolvable problem. Their solution can fix a localized problem for a small period and be declared a "success" allowing everyone to go on about their lives. The problem of homelessness is so diverse, complex and enormous that any effort tends to be like a drop in the ocean, and people quickly become discouraged. We need to find the will to continue adding drops.

It needs to be recognized that there is not a "simple" one-size-fits-all solution to homelessness. Some are homeless because they want to be and they consider themselves morally superior to you. Many have problems with addiction. In almost all cases trying to help will come across as patronizing and will continue to alienate them. If they are desperate enough then they will accept this until it becomes normal and eventually a lifestyle. What has happened is that each side considers the other as not like them. Us and them. 

What is rarely offered to the homeless is community. For that we require "them" to become like "us". They have to smell like us, and act like us and have the stuff we have before we can associate with them as equals. Any relationship where one side considers themselves morally superior to the other and hands them things out of pity will eventually become abusive and harmful to both sides. 

The tragedy of it is that while we are occasionally willing to attempt to make "them" like "us", we are unwilling to really associate with them unless they have done so. We are also unwilling to change ourselves to be more like "them" because in our pride we think our lifestyle is morally superior. If our own comfort and wealth are more important than anothers life are we really superior?

The "creative" solution to homelessness would be the one where both sides believe that they are part of the same whole. If part is broken then heal that part instead of isolating it. If it is painful then endure that pain and accept that cost so long as "we" are broken.... and we have always been broken.
