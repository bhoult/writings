# Would becoming homeless be a good strategy to cut costs?

I did this for two years once. Lived in a ford explorer while working as a programmer in Little Rock, AR. My reasoning was that I had a lot of debt to pay off and this would save me about $7000 a year. It did, I paid off the debt and it was not that unpleasant. If you want details and advice I wrote about it here: Minimal Intentions

I think in retrospect and after reading the responses here the key element seems to be if you are doing it by choice and see it as an adventure of sorts or if you were forced into it and therefore see it as a failure. Your perspective is really more important than your circumstances. 

I also watched this TED talk: The year I was homeless. She started by choice but gradually adopted a negative attitude from the circumstances that caused it to became very unhealthy over time. 

If you read all the responses here, you will note that some tried it and thought it was great while others thought it was terrible. Consider whether your reasoning going into it resembles one side or the other, your experience will likely be similar. Also recognize how much you care about others opinions of your life choices, how much you internalize those opinions. Some people will invariably have a negative opinion about your choice... will that matter to you?
