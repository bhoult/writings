# How much money do I need to thru hike the Appalachian Trail?

I did it for about $4000. I know two girls that did it for less than $1000 each. I met one guy that just started walking in jeans and a t-shirt with no money and picked up what he needed along the way. 

Basically, as Hans pointed out, the more you spend the more comfortable it is. For $4000 I shared a hotel room about once every two weeks, and ate all I wanted when I got to town. I was not able to afford to change out my equipment much after I started though, which was unfortunate since I started with heavy stuff. 

$3000+ should be fairly comfortable if you are frugal and take less than 7 months. Less than $1000 would be risky and kind of unpleasant. For more details, see my answer below.
