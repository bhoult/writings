# How can someone who has nothing change their life?

Three guidelines come to mind.

Become excellent at something other people need but few people can do well. This should be something that is difficult for you but you can spend a lifetime improving. If it is too easy you will become bored with it. It should be something you can become better at than 99.9% of the people around you. When you are needed you can name your price.Build something. By this I mean take what you have and use it to go to the next level. Think of a wall, bricks stacked on bricks. Great things are created a little at a time, with dedication, care, persistence. In a consumer society we are trained that solutions are disposable. We start from the beginning over and over never progressing. Find something started that you can build on. Then build on again. Look for the hard slow path…everyone else is busy running in tight little circles around fast and easy. Greatness is always found where nobody is looking.Fail well, and often. Fail gloriously at things others pretend not to care about. If you don't fail then you were too timid to try. If you don't try you will never get beyond where you started. Failure is the single greatest skill you can possess. Success is the end of the road. If you stop there then your goal was unworthy.
