# What is the most minimalistic way to live one's life?

First imagine a minimalist life. Hold in your mind an image of what could be. When you find yourself dealing with the many little irritations in life, consider if you could get rid of the source of the issue instead of maintaining it. What would you do without that thing? How would you spend the time saved? 

Recognize the consumerist patterns. A product for every problem. Look for the truth behind why you buy what you buy before you buy it. Try to go without for a while, impulse buys are a trained behavior that is not for your benefit. Consider your true needs: air, water, food, shelter. Everything beyond this is unnecessary. If it does not enhance your life then leave it be.

You are born with a limited time on a beautiful earth. Your only real resource that has value is that time. Recognize it's worth and don't exchange it for trivialities. Be skeptical of those that offer you various forms of dirt to enslave a portion of your life. Question the patterns that society teaches. Support those that have real worth and just observe and understand the rest, leave them in peace. Appreciate those things that you don't own or control, they will be with you forever in memory and will make no further demands.

Try to adapt to what is instead of forcing the world to adapt to your preconceptions. Don't make progress dependent on things that you have little influence over. Blaming externals is small comfort in retrospect. Forgive and keep moving.

I wish I had heeded my own advice earlier in life.
