# I am going backpacking on the Appalachian trail next week. What are some ways to help me fall asleep in my tent? I always have trouble falling asleep.

It is unlikely you will have the same trouble sleeping after hiking all day every day for any period of time. I have trouble sleeping normally, but when I was on the AT it was all I could do most nights to eat dinner. Often I was asleep as soon as I got into bed.

I used a hammock most of the time Hennessy Hammock: The Coolest Tent in the World if you have not tried one, you may want to look into it. They can be a lot cooler and more comfortable than a tent, but some people just can't get used to them.

Basically get a good air-pad, and a sufficiently warm sleeping bag and you should be ok after the first several nights. Some ibuprofen will probably help if you are too sore to sleep.
