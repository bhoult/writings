# Should I learn HTML and CSS when there are plenty of tools such as WordPress, Drupal are available?

If you have an idea of the finished product in your head then by limiting yourself to high level tools such as WordPress and drupal you will essentially be asking yourself "how do I need to compromise on my idea in order to make it work with these prebuilt tools". You will spend your time looking for the cms, themes, and modules that are most similar to what you want, and you will compromise your vision to make it fit the tool.

The more you learn about the basics, the less you will have to limit yourself to these constraints. You will be able to come closer to your original vision by customizing the appropriate pieces. In this case the html and css.
