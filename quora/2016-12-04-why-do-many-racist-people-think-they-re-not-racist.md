# Why do many racist people think they're not racist?

I am a racist. I like people who are similar to me more than I like people who are different. I am a tribalist, I associate with people I see around me every day more than I do with those far away. I am an ageist. I don't relate as well to those older than me, I don't understand those younger. I am a sexist, a bigot, a nationalist, a hypocrite.

It is from within me that injustice is born. In a moment of weakness I let it out into the world. Because of my shame I find someone else to blame it on.

As long as I remember that I can fight my nature. Try to be better. Those that deny their racism need not struggle against their lesser selves. They have the ironic luxury of believing themselves better than that… better than me. I hope they truly are.

I want to be part of the good people. While the world falls apart, I want to say it is not my fault, not my friends fault, not my nations fault. It is the bad people, the ones I don't know. I want it to be simple, the complexity of their lives reduced to labels that I can distance myself from. I want them to look different, speak different, dress different so I don't accidentally see my humanity in their eyes.

The world is fractured. Some welcome this and believing themselves wronged turn to hatred and blame. Some struggle against it and humbly war with their own nature. The rest will sit on the sidelines reassuring each other that it is not their problem, after all, they are not racists. They are so much better than those poor ignorant people.
