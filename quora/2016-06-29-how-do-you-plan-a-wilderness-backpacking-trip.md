# How do you plan a wilderness backpacking trip?

Think of all the things you will need apart from food and water then put them in a backpack. Stuff not fitting? Re-evaluate “need” then take some stuff out. Repeat until everything fits.

Weigh the bag. If it is more than 20lbs then re-evaluate “need” and take some stuff out. Repeat until it is less than 20lbs. Keep repeating until it is as far under 20lbs as you can reasonably go. Consider replacing heavy stuff if you can afford it.

Believe it or not this is doable. Lots of people do it. The people that do it the most are the people that hike the most. I know you are probably ok with more weight, but it makes the difference between enjoying the hike and enduring it. If you are with others then think about reducing redundancy if possible. You do not all need first aid kits and water filters.

Add food and water for the number of days you will be hiking. If it is over 35lbs then get lighter food if possible. Focus on stuff with lots of calories for the weight, bring some squeeze butter or olive / coconut oil to add more calories. Lipton sides / ramen / mac & cheese / instant mashed potatoes are all good cheap staples. Mountain house freeze-dried is good if you can afford it. Don’t worry that much about taste, everything tastes good after hiking all day. Also bring a big bag of gorp to snack on.

After that if it is an established trail then get some kind of guidebook. This should tell you where you can expect to re-supply with water / food and will let you know how much elevation change you can expect between points. Plan a few days in advance but be willing to change plans, things happen, don’t get stressed out over keeping a schedule.

If you are with a group it is unlikely your pace will be the same for everybody. So pick spots to meet up then “hike your own hike”.
