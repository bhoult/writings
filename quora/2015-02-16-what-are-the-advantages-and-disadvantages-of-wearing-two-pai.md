# What are the advantages and disadvantages of wearing two pairs of socks when hiking?

As has been mentioned, the first sock should be a thin synthetic like a nylon dress sock or panty-hose. The second should be thick wool. My favorite are bridgedale, they lasted over 2000 miles on the AT where smartwool fell apart in 400 miles or so. Look for a fine tight weave without a bunch of fibers that you can pull off. Bring an extra pair of thick socks that you can wear at night while your others dry. The thin sock will dry quickly so you only really need one pair.

Advantages: less blisters, drier feet, less pain.
Disadvantages: less bragging about how many blisters you had and the pain you endured. Fewer masochistic photo opportunities.
