# What are some great healthy foods for trekking and traveling?

Typically stuff that is light and normally considered "bad for you", high in fat, starch, salt, calories. If you are like me then also relatively cheap. When hiking long distances I normally take instant mashed potatoes, ramen, lipton sides, soup mixes. Then a small bottle of olive oil and spices. If it is not really hot adding summer sausage and cheese will let you greatly improve all of the above. Tuna packets are good for protein. Gorp or cookies to snack on while you walk.... look for the ones with the highest calorie to weight ratio. Fig Newtons are surprisingly unhealthy... thus good for hiking. Also small candy bars are good if it is not so hot that they melt. Peanut butter is also very high in calories, and good for protein.

If you can dehydrate stuff before you go then bring stuff that you can add to the above for variety. Onions, collard greens, tomatoes or whatever you like. You don't need a lot, it is just to add flavor.

In the first couple days you can bring stuff that is heavy or not so hiker-friendly. Like bagels, or hot dogs. Just eat it quick before it spoils, gets mutilated or attracts animals. If you are in bear country then bring a "bear bag" and hang your food from a tree at night.

You can get a lot of your calories from drinks. I use a 32oz nalgene bottle. You can mix by shaking and you can pour boiling water directly in the bottle. Also they are easier to clean because of the wide mouth. Be careful with hot liquids though if there is any air inside when you shake the bottle it will build up quite a bit of pressure. Easiest solution is to put the lid on loosely then shake lightly for a while to allow the air to heat without being sealed. Then tighten, shake vigorously and carefully open. Use empty two-liter soda bottles for water storage. Nalgene bottles are kind of heavy so you only want one. Dried soup base or bullion also make a good drink, and has lots of salt. In the summer bring lots of Gatorade mix for the calories and the salts. In the winter hot Gatorade is also good. Hot chocolate has a good calorie to weight ratio it is good to drink before going to bed. Coffee or tea is good in the morning. Bring some sugar or honey to add... add lots. Also if you are really skinny (no body fat) then try drinking a mouthful of olive oil before you start up a hill. 

If money is not a concern then the various freeze dried mountain house meals are light and very tasty.
