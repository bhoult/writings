# Can it be possible that God exists just because we believe he exists?

Money exists because it is a piece of paper with the correct pictures on it. It has power because people believe it does and they act in accordance with that belief. Its existence and its power are completely different issues. People individually have a small amount of power, their ability to effect change over the length of their life. An idea (belief) can harness a portion of many individuals power to give it more ability than any individual. It has a great deal of power but no existence.

God exists or does not regardless of the amount of power his idea has at any given moment. "Miracles" as proof of God's existence claim to demonstrate power beyond human capability regardless of the magnitude of the aggregate human potential. Humans in whatever quantity acting together in common belief cannot raise the dead... although they can launch a space shuttle or build a pyramid.

Religion is a type of idea that harnesses human power for good (communal/inclusive) or evil (individual/exclusive) ends. It does this by claiming a diety exist and that its individual power exceeds the aggregate of all human power. They further claim that the religious leaders know what it wants, live according to that knowledge and are able to speak for it. In so doing they inspire human power to greatness or destruction, likely depending on how much they believe they will actually be accountable to the deity they claim to represent.

I personally find it alarming how readily religious leaders are willing to put words in the mouth of a God who they claim will control their eternal destiny. I suspect that those that do so easily comprehend the human power in religion more than the actual existence of God.
