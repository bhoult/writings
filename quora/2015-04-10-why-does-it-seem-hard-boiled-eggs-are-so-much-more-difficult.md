# Why does it seem hard-boiled eggs are so much more difficult to peel now than they were 40 years ago?

Try making them in a pressure cooker. There is a air pocket in one end of the egg that will expand when the pressure cooker releases its pressure, the air pocket separates the egg from the shell as it expands.
