# How would you survive if, for the next 24 hours, everyone on Earth tried to hunt you down and kill you? You have a five hour head-start. They can use anything at their disposal like dogs, militaries, napalm, etc. The World will work together.

Walk into the woods with shovel, knife, rope, zip-ties shotgun and bottle of water. Find a tree without a tap-root (maples, ash, cottonwood), preferably a dead one by a stream or lake. Keep walking as far as possible then remove and burn all clothing, smear mud over self, backtrack to tree. Dig a hole under the trunk big enough to climb into. Hide fresh dirt and signs of activity as much as possible.

Climb in hole for the next 24 hours.

If anyone finds me then point shotgun at them so I can tie them up with the rope. If they make noise then use knife. Otherwise let them go after 24 hour period.
