# Why is backpacking more popular nowadays?

A caged bird is moved to a window. The door is opened for the first time in years. The bird, built to fly free, remains immobile.

I sit in a rectangular room imagining a caged bird briefly before I move to a small mobile rectangle that will carry me to another larger rectangle.

Our grandparents found brief refuge from the world in these boxes, but now we live in them. The cage is becoming more obvious.
