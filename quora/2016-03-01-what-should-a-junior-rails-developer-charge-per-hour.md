# What should a junior rails developer charge per hour?

I generally charge $50/hour in a smallish town in Arkansas. But I have about 10 years of experience and am a "full stack" developer. I would probably charge double that if I had to live in a large city.

I also consider that to be working hours in something I am already competent at so I will not have to spend a lot of time learning. If I have to learn something really strange and only applicable to that project then I would likely bill full hours, but if it was something common that a "full stack" developer should already know then I would probably bill fewer hours than I actually work.

I consider my competition in this area to be college student types, who may only bill $10/hour but will be mostly learning and will likely produce a sub-optimal product. So I am essentially trying to convince my customers that it would be less expensive in the long run to pay me five times what they would pay some random college kid because I will produce a better product in 1/5th the time. Some of that due to experience, some due to Rails.

Were I in your position, I would maybe start at $30/hr and if you get more work than you can do then raise your prices until you only get what you can handle.
