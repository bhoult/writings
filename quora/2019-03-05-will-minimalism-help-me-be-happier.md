# Will minimalism help me be happier?

I wanted to say yes. After all that is where we want the path to lead. But the truth is no… it has not. It merely lightens the load you must carry while you chase happiness.

Then when you catch it, you just see it fade away. Happiness is not ours to own. You may try to imprison happiness in possessions. It always dies when trapped in such a way.

Learn to chase happiness. Always. It is a game the living play. Live lightly, run fast.
