# How can I go about finding a thru-hiking partner?

Turns out that over a long distance it is really hard to hike with someone that goes a different pace than you. If you are obligated to stay with a friend of yours then you will have to deal with this… since you are looking for someone then it is not a problem.

I would suggest that if it is a popular trail like the AT then just show up and start hiking. You will meet lots of people if you start in May sometime, some of them you will keep running into at shelters and in towns and since these people are already going your pace you will probably become friends. It is alarmingly easy to make friends while hiking. You have everything in common with everyone else out there. You all went through similar things to be there, you are all thinking about the same things (food, gear) all day long.

If you are going on a less traveled trail then it will be harder. Whoever you find, determine in advance how you will deal with the pacing issue, otherwise it can lead to frustration and resentment. Not a good frame of mind to be in if you want to enjoy your hike.
