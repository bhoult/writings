# Coding is like cooking. Coding is like writing an essay. What are some other coding analogies?

Coding is like grasping a fist full of lightning and bending it to your will. At your command it will flash throughout the world bringing you hidden insights and images of far away places. It will relentlessly bow to your every whim. Like a harnessed god it will toil outside of the restraints of time and space. With it you command forces beyond comprehension. An army of light.

It is the dream of oracles, the spells of sorcerers and wizards come to life. For this kings would have once killed and died. It is the spirit of the golem giving life to inert clay. It is the gatekeeper to a library of Alexandria, reborn to span the earth. It is the chariot of Apollo, the lightning of Zeus.

Through coding we harness the collective will of all mankind. We tell them what to believe, what to buy, how to think. Like the sirens of Odysseus, we lure them from reality into a world of fantasy where they can revel in the delusions of pretentious gods. Power beyond the most ambitious imagining of our ancestors harnessed to cat videos, pornography and mindless entertainment.

Coding is like angry children playing with nuclear weapons. A power we are unfit to wield… and yet we do.
