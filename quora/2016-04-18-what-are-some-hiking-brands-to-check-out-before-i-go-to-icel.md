# What are some hiking brands to check out before I go to Iceland?

I tend to buy Lowa boots, but there are many good brands these days. The thing to look for if you are dealing with sharp rocks is you want it to have a stiff sole. You should barely be able to bend them with your hands. However, make sure you break them in for a few months before you do any serious walking.

If you don't get stiff enough soles then consider getting some insoles. Superfeet makes some nice ones that will last a long time.
