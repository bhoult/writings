# How can someone survive in the wild?

Water, shelter, food. Put all your thought and energy into securing those in that order. When you have achieved that for the near future then security, community, redundancy. Try to form partnerships with others that contribute value. Avoid those that don't and are unwilling to change. When you have assets hide them in different places. Minimize how much you are noticed and maximize how much you notice. Adapt. Don't ever let yourself become complacent. Consider sacrifice as an option, you will not be able to control your circumstances to the extent you did in the past. Be willing to lose so you can try again. Be patient and cautious, injury will be expensive. Minimize energy expenditure, do everything slowly, deliberately, carefully. If in doubt, find another way. Do nothing until you have carefully considered it, your chances will be limited, panic will likely be fatal.
