# How would a minimalist organize one's daily life?

Most people, when confronted by a desire, ask themselves "what do I need to add in order to attain this goal?" A frugal individual also considers the cost and asks if the desire is really worth attaining. A minimalist will additionally recognize that his time, space and energy are finite. So what is gained must necessarily displace what is already there. This then must be considered as part of the cost. He must also consider what can be removed so that the thing desired can achieve its full potential.
