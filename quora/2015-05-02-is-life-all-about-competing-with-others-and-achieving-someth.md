# Is life all about competing with others and achieving something big or is it OK to sit back, relax and have a simple life?

For you at this point it is about answering this question. Unfortunately all anyone can do is tell you how they answered it for themselves. Because the majority care more about what they seem to be than who they are, most of what they tell you will be lies. Judge them by their humility.

At some point you will have to choose an answer that you can only dimly perceive, or you will grow old at the crossroads and lose both options. Then you will learn if you have the courage to reach for it, the discipline to not forget, the stamina to not give up. A successful life is one lived consistently with your nearest approach to truth.
