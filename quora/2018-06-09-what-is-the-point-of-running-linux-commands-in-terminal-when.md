# What is the point of running Linux commands in terminal when GUI is available?

Long ago there were a few rare people that understood computers and all the rest that did not even comprehend why they might want to think about them. Those that understood “spoke” to them using words in a language much like I am speaking to you now in written English. This was and is the command line interface.

The problem was how to make it so the illiterate could use the computer without screwing it up. So they reduced language to a bunch of little pictures that could be poked at.

This was not an improvement, it was a regression to accommodate ignorance and laziness.

If you find yourself in a foreign land where nobody speaks your language you will suddenly find your communicative abilities reduced to point and grunt. Your question is like asking why that society continues to use language instead of everyone just pointing and grunting like you.
