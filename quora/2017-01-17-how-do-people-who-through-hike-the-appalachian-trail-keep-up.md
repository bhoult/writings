# How do people who through-hike the Appalachian Trail keep up with their money?

$3000 divided by 2200 miles = approximately $1.36 per mile.

I leave town… walk 38 miles, I have a $51 budget for this town.

I get to the nearest restaurant and completely disregard that while I binge eat before passing out somewhere. But somewhere in the back of my head I was thinking $51…

I planned to spend $3000, I actually spent about $4000. But to answer your question I did not really “keep up” with my money, but I did keep in mind a rough estimate on how much I should spend, and how much I was over-budget. If I was getting too over-budget I would shorten the amount of time I spent in town.
