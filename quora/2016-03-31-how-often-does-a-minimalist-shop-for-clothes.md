# How often does a minimalist shop for clothes?

You buy clothes in proportion to how much you need them and how important they are to you. Minimalism is about prioritizing the important and reducing the unused. If you use all the clothes you have then keep them. If clothes are a high priority in your life and you want to buy some then go ahead. Don't make minimalism into legalism. This is about optimization not necessarily deprivation.

Personally, clothes mean next to nothing to me. I consider them just another way of lying to others about who I am. I would run around naked except that I would get arrested for it. I have considered making two indestructible robes out of something like spectra (10 times stronger than steel) that would last the rest of my life and would allow me to get rid of all my clothes. I would be seen as a bit of a freak, but I am not sure how much I care. I currently have about two weeks worth of clothes that works out to be optimal in doing laundry. I wear them until there are more holes than fabric. Everything I have is either black or white or some shade of gray so I don't have to bother with matching. I buy a new piece of something about once a year to replace something else that has rotted to the point of indecency.
