# Who will survive longer without any food, a very fat person or a very muscled person?

If they both sit in a room, tied to a chair staring at each other then the fat person would live the longest… muscles use energy and fat supplies energy. Although I believe water is necessary to metabolize fat so we are assuming that they both have access to water.

In a more “realistic” situation… like say being lost in a forest, or trying to survive the zombie apocalypse, the muscled person has more ability to find food, and thus a greater ability to survive. If it comes down to it, then the muscled person will be eating the fat person and will therefore survive longer.
