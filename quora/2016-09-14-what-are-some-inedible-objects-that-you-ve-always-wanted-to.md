# What are some inedible objects that you've always wanted to eat?

An alien… Like the kind from outer space. Assuming one exists.

I mean we are genetically similar to every other living thing on this planet. That has to play a part in flavor. But grilled alien would be something else entirely. It would literally taste like nothing else we have ever tried, like something out of this world.
