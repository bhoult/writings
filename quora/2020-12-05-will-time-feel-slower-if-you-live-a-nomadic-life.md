# Will time feel slower if you live a nomadic life?

I think so. My observation is that repetitive behavior blends together over time and becomes similar to a single event.

When we were children the days were long. The span between getting out of school and going to bed was near eternal. We were living in our imagination where the landscape was fluid.

As we got older each day became more like the last. We worked, we watched some forgettable tv show, we slept. We saw the same people, did the same things. Nothing significant separated the day from the last. If we wonder what did we do last week, it's probably much the same as the week before that, or a year ago, or next week.

Our perception of time is compressed.

15 years ago I changed everything and hiked the Appalachian trail for 7 months. That period still seems more relevant than the intervening years. For a time everything was new, each day was exciting. Then even that became a routine.

Becoming nomadic will bring a degree of newness to each day. You will see different things, deal with new situations, look forward to each coming day. But eventually patterns will form, and the days will start to shorten.

A day relived a million times is just a day. May you instead have many days.
