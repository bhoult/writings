# What is the point of living if we are going to die and not remember anything?

? - birth - {life} - death - ?

It depends on if you are a glass half empty type. The truth is that nobody knows what the (?) represents, although everybody makes a guess. It is intolerable to leave this unknown since we know we will someday arrive there. If you manage to convince yourself it is oblivion then life is suspended disconnected in that void. If you convince yourself it is God then life is encapsulated in that narrative. In both cases you are pretending to know the unknown, some call this faith. Your angst is as arbitrary as the “coping mechanism” you despise.

If you choose you can stare into the glory of the night sky and be humbled by how little we know of any of this. This adventure of sailing into mystery with hope and courage. But despite your ignorance you instead choose to cast judgement, you condemn the known because of the unknowable.

What we can know is that we did not earn this life we have. It is a gift. You could appreciate what you were given, what you did nothing to deserve, or whine that you were not given more of what you think you want. That choice is yours.

It is also worth noting that in a practical sense you will never experience non-existance. The source of your misery is as imaginary and hypothetical a construct as the gods you despise. You see others go through death, you envision your own mortality as a result. You are driven to jealousy of those younger and more vital than yourself but are powerless to take what they have, so you despair. Have you heard the story of the dog that dropped his bone in the water?

Unable to face failure you declare the gift meaningless. This is the response of a spoiled child.

If you cannot bear to look upon the infinite, which is not for you to control, then instead limit yourself to the practical. In that realm “eternity” is the span between your birth and death, reality is a construct of your mind. That is all you will ever experience. All of “meaning” is contained therein, and is yours to do with as you will.
