# Can someone with three bikes be a minimalist? If so, how would you explain it to someone who believes it makes you a hypocrite?

Generally a minimalist tries to have what they use and nothing more. The key word in this is tries. Your concern over hypocrisy implies that anyone who has anything cannot be a minimalist because there remains something that can be discarded.

Therefore, minimalism is an ideal that is resisted by functionality and practicality. A minimalist tries to find the balance at which their life is optimally lived. Somewhere between having nothing and having so much that it becomes a liability. This is a personal choice and should not require justification.

If you are accused of hypocrisy point out that the ideals and priorities by which you valuate the things you choose to keep may differ from those of your accuser. The principal of removing the unnecessary as a personal discipline still remains.

As for how to respond, there is no need. Those that make unfounded accusations have already exposed their ignorance. Their misunderstanding does not merit validation.
