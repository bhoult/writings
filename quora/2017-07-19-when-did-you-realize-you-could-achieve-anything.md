# When did you realize you could achieve anything?

I realized this as a small child. And then I grew up a little, and realized how full of crap I was.

There is much you can achieve. Maybe more than you realize. If you are persistent you can achieve great things, more than society thinks is possible. But this is not “anything”. Depending on your ambitions success comes after hundreds, or even thousands of failures. This is how you achieve something… you fail a lot and keep trying.

But make sure your target is possible… I want to barbeque an alien after I hijack his flying saucer. I want to urinate on the surface of the surface of the sun. I want to eat Cheetos while I watch the universe burn out in trillions of years.

I will assuredly fail.
