# What is the best way to help a beggar?

Maybe get to know them, sit with them for a while and listen to what they have to say. Avoid telling them what they should do unless they ask. Just ask questions and let them talk and be heard. Try to meet them half way. 

Maybe ask instead what you can learn from your new friend, how did they get there. Think about your assumptions. Don't try to turn them into what you think they should be. Find out what they want to be and think about if you can support that. By this point you will know how to answer your original question. Then it is just a matter of deciding if you will.
