# Is Linux a poor man's macOS?

I would think it more accurate to say OSX is a hipsters linux.

Quora seems to think the above needs more explanation... so here it is:

OSX is a limited version of Unix. Basically they lock you into their vision of what Unix should be and then overcharge you to participate in that. To be fair their control over the hardware and software does yield some benefits in things "just working". However it is more like they graciously allow you to borrow their stuff if you can afford to buy into their elite club. Then they dictate what you are allowed to do with their stuff. 

Some people don't care so long as it gets the job done. Others see it as more of a fashion statement than a tool. I judge my tools on how well they accomplish what I want them to do. Apple and Microsoft do the same with their tools... the ones purchasing their products.
