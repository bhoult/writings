# Should we live as frugally and simply as possible such that we can donate as much money to charity as possible?

I also struggle with this question. At the moment I believe the answer is simply yes we should. But no I won't mainly because I place my own temporary comfort over the lives of others. 

It is kind of like a magic trick, I know this to be true because my past and present actions confirm it, but I have to force myself to consider it because my mind will immediately fabricate excuses and distractions. 

Today I chose to buy a cup of coffee for $3... I could have prevented a child in Africa from starving to death. Instead I chose to not think about that since it is easy to ignore if it is far away... And I don't look. 

I could say it is too big a problem and not my responsibility. But I know that we could prevent starvation worldwide for the budget of a major motion picture. But we don't. We never have, and I along with the rest of humanity are broken because of it. We are not "good"... Neither am I good if I am honest. But neither am I honest most of the time, which is how I live with myself.
