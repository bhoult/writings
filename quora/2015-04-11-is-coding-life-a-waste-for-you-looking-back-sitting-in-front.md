# Is coding life a waste for you looking back (sitting in front of a PC all the time)?

If you are just "sitting in front of a PC all the time" then it would be kind of a waste. Most of the time though there is a real world reason to be sitting in front of that PC. The coding part is just a means to an end.

Sometimes the end is kind of meaningless, sometimes it is just to make a living, but if that is what it becomes then that was your choice. Coding is unique in that something you create can be almost instantly distributed throughout the world and improve the lives of its users. You may never be recognized for it or compensated for it and it may only be a small thing but if a small thing is duplicated a million times can you say it was a "waste".

If there is a big meaningful thing that you are capable of doing then by all means, do that! Until then do the best you can at what you are doing.
