# What can I survive without?

Just about everything. Survival is a really low bar to measure against. Basically you need food, water, air and some kind of shelter. Everything else is optional. Rodents can do this. Insects that don't even have a brain can do it.

In addition to the above the only thing that is really essential is the will to survive the ability to adapt and the clarity to see things as they really are.

To put it another way the thing that will kill you is self pity over your condition not being what you believe you deserve accompanied by an unwillingness to change that. This is generally accomplished by blaming circumstances on something other than yourself that you can't do anything about.

Either that or by taking risks that are unjustified by the reward or your actual ability.

What you can't survive without? Humility.
