# If the U.S. were invaded and its armed forces decimated, how well could the civilian population defend itself with its guns?

“Decimated” literally means to remove (kill) one in ten. So if the armed forces were decimated then they would still remain at 90% of their former strength.

According to this: List of countries by Military Strength Index - Wikipedia The US would still be at the top of the list with a strength of 0.846 post decimation.

I expect the civilian population would still be sitting around stockpiling .22 ammo.
