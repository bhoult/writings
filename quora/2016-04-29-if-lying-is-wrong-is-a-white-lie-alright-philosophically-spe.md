# If lying is wrong, is a white lie alright? (Philosophically speaking)

Lying is a form of manipulation. It is an attempt to influence someones decisions through giving them false information. A "white lie" is just giving them false information "for their own good". It assumes that the person to whom you are lying is incompetent and unable to make good decisions for themselves with true information.

If the person you are lying to is your friend or is an adult then you are presuming to be more capable of making decisions effecting their lives than they are. If a person did this to me I would assume they thought I was an idiot and I would re-evaluate our relationship based on that. It is very presumptive and insulting to assume that you know what is best for another person more than they do.

If your goal is to intentionally harm through manipulation then lies can be justified... like hiding Jews from the Nazis as another commenter mentioned. In this case you have no obligation to respect the Nazi. He is your enemy. Tell him lies.

You may also believe that it is justified to lie to children to protect them or as an expedient way to get them to do what you want. In most cases I would think that saying nothing at all would be better. In the long run they will find out that you lie and will not respect what you say as a result.

"White lies" are just normal lies with added arrogance.
