# What is so great about minimalism?

There are lots of benefits (pros) to minimalism but also lots of cons. In making a list of this type you challenge the person you are discussing it with to negate your list and produce a list of their own which they will be obliged to defend. If you want a rousing debate then this is the way to go. 

In keeping with the whole minimalist philosophy though I would propose that we agree on one simple truth, an axiom that will define the finite domain of the discussion. Then we can put all our various beliefs, ideas and arguments into that framework and work together to reduce and purify the concept instead of coming up with an endlessly growing jumble of benefits.

So... here is the simple truth on which I hope we can agree: We are going to die.

This changes the concept from an open ended perspective of what I want and like now, to what will I make room for in the limited time I have. In an unlimited life there is no cost to time. Get what you want when you want it makes sense, you always get a do-over. In a limited lifespan we can't have it all though... the box is finite. When you fill it, you will have to take something out to add another thing in.

Think of time as the length of the box, resources as the width, wisdom as the depth. You don't get to see the end but you know it is coming and the older you get the nearer it becomes. You have some influence on the other two aspects, but even a large box can be quickly filled with worthless things.

The benefits of a minimalist lifestyle is that you consciously choose what you include in your box, your life. You leave out the meaningless so that you can make room for what has real value so that when you reach the end you will not find it wasted. Your list of benefits are what remains when all the worthless has been removed.
