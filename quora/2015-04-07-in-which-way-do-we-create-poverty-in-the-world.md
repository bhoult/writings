# In which way do we create poverty in the world?

I would think that "wealth" is created by the production of material goods that enhance our lives over a time period. If a tool allows for a reduction of labor to accomplish a task then it's value is the time saved over the life of the tool. The truly wealthy have the freedom to apply their time according to their free will.

If this is the case then poverty could be "created" through the destruction of wealth. For example through war or theft. Or poverty could be created by reducing the benefit of wealth through corruption, or compulsion. Perhaps poverty can be "created" by producing an artificial desire for goods that make an individual less productive or effective such as drugs, mindless entertainment and debt slavery. 

If by whatever means you can deprive an individual or society of hope and the belief that they have the ability to make things better for tomorrow than they are today then you have successfully "created" poverty.
