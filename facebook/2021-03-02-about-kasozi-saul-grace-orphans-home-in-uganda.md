# About Kasozi Saul (Grace Orphans Home in Uganda)

About Kasozi Saul (Grace Orphans Home in Uganda)
(Probably a Scam)
https://graceorphanshome.org/

Since the fundraiser for Nelsons children a large number of individuals and organizations have contacted me asking for help.  This is to be expected since the need is enormous and real.  Unfortunately without being able to verify what is happening many asking for help may be professional con artists.

Kasozi was very persistent so I thought he was either genuine and very dedicated, or a scammer.

I contacted a bunch of random individuals in the same city Mityana, Uganda.  One lady took the day off work and hired a motorcycle to take her to where the orphanage was supposed to be.

From what she found, there was no actual person or orphanage there, a few people knew Kasozi but did not know he had anything to do with an orphanage.  If anyone wants further details please let me know.

So, basically it is not 100% but seems likely that this is a scam.  I know Kasozi has contacted several individuals on my friend list so hopefully you have not donated anything yet.

There is an orphanage in Kenya that Bruce Dickey used to work with and that he visited personally several times.  That one is legitimate.  I will get more information on it if anyone wants to work with them.
