# --- An Appeal to Educators ---

--- An Appeal to Educators ---

By someone who has spent a lifetime coding, and is now learning again

We have started using AI tools heavily at work in the last few months in the programming industry. About half the people I have spoken with about it are resistant for a variety of reasons. I started out in that camp to some degree because it does not take much effort to extrapolate my obsolescence in the near future.

There is a kind of dismay when you realize that a skill set you spent 40 years developing is suddenly 90% obsolete in the course of a year. The usual stages of grief commence.  Anger and denial are certainly there. Then like the Luddites of old we have to decide if we want to resist a reality beyond our power to stop, or do that which is still in our power and adapt ourselves to deal with the coming changes.

If you choose to adapt it has to be done as objectively as possible. There is a tendency to anticipate disaster.  We humans we have been rubber-necking tragedy and dreaming about it in stories and legends for our entire existence... it helps (to a degree) to anticipate what may come. But it is important that we not become one sided and cease to dream of what could be, while we fear a merely "potential" future. Loss of jobs, changing of culture, a lack of security because of the rate of change, we no longer have the comfort of believing we can see what is coming and prepare.

Personally I am throwing myself into using the best tools available to solve the problems in front of me. I am probably several times as productive as a result and am finding that without having to deal with the myriad of tiny details that I am rediscovering the joy that originally got me programming in the first place, solving real problems. The path I had walked had become tedious, if lucrative after so many years. Now it may not be lucrative much longer but it is a joy to travel, like riding in an air conditioned vehicle, dreaming of tomorrow after pulling a hand cart for so long just to deal with today.

The future will have to learn how to solve human problems with every available tool. Our track record has not been so great in that regard. Maybe the current trend will exceed our capacity to adapt. It is happening very fast. The transition will likely be traumatic for those of us living through it. But we can still choose the path based on what we hope for or what we fear.  We currently face a multitude of problems for which the old solutions have proven inadequate.  There will not be a shortage of problems to solve.

I would urge the teachers in this thread to guide your students appropriately. Give them tools to excel in the environment that they will find themselves. Some of our old worldview will still be valid.  It can and should be preserved.  but we will have to carefully and humbly re-evaluate and choose what to keep. 

Because we will never again live in the world we grew up in, but this new world will still need teachers.
