# This moment has been lost. Ignored in favor of past regrets and future fears. He

This moment has been lost. Ignored in favor of past regrets and future fears. Here and now where I find my closest approach to reality I am distracted.  My leash beeping reminders of failures in far off places. Events that have passed or might come to be. Fantasies and fears not even my own. My mind is not where I am, when I am.

In this moment, this place.

Nothing is broken.

So it fades into the background, unappreciated while I anxiously turn away.
