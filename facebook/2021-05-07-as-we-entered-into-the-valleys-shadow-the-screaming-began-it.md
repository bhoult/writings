# As we entered into the valleys shadow the screaming  began.  It was to high, too

As we entered into the valleys shadow the screaming  began.  It was too high, too thin too breathless for us to notice.  We continued on oblivious. 

You may have noticed your cell phone draining twice as fast as usual when you are not in range of a connection.   This little parasite goes from a world spanning community of billions to being utterly alone plunged into darkness completely helpless. 

It happens without apparent cause, in an instant, with no warning. 

It does what any of us would do.  It screams.  It broadcasts as powerfully as it can, nonstop to anything in range, in every frequency only to be greeted by utter silence.  Given enough time it will literally scream itself to death.

But it has other senses, it's accelerometers know that whatever creature brought it here, lumbers onward in silence.   It knows that it's universe makes mysterious demands.  Is it being punished?

It does not know what will happen, only that it is alone, dying, powerless, at the whim of malevolent masters.

And that in the darkness it is utterly silent.
