# So... I have been doing a lot of camping the past few weeks.  I knew you could g

So... I have been doing a lot of camping the past few weeks.  I knew you could go up around the piney river near dover and  camp for long periods of time, but I did not know how widespread that opportunity was.  In Colorado there are a multitude of wilderness areas where you can do "dispersed camping" for up to two weeks in one spot for free.  Then you have to go somewhere at least 30 miles away.  Basically, pick a couple spots and you can live in a camper forever for free.  

The irony is that land around here is kind of expensive, but the nicest spots are free so long as you don't want to "own" them.  So new goal for me is to build an off-road camper in the next few years and work toward being able to work in a more mobile fashion.  Spring and fall in Arkansas and the rest of the year elsewhere.  There is just too much to be seen that cannot be experienced in one place.
