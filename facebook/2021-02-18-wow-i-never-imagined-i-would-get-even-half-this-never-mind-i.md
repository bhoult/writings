# Wow... I never imagined I would get even half this.  Never mind in six hours.  I

Wow... I never imagined I would get even half this.  Never mind in six hours.  I am guilty of hoping for the best in people, but nevertheless expecting to be disappointed.  

For me, these children and the people of the Pokot tribe who are being systematically murdered this will make a huge difference.  Not just from the money, but from the knowledge that the world has not forgotten them in their trouble.

With this I believe the children will be securely able to attend boarding school for the next year and their supplies will be covered as well.  It is hard to imagine what a difference this will make in their lives.  The future is in the hands of children such as this, the effects of your generosity will survive the death of Nelson.

They only have two seasons in Kenya, rainy and dry. So they will be at school for three months then home one month throughout the year.  When they are home they will be living with their nearest relative Nelson's sister Catherine Kordado. 

She has her own family, and I am sure did not foresee her life going the way it has.  Whatever else is raised will be given to her to help support these kids.

I will probably do this again in a year.  I will print some calendars or prints of my photos to see if I can sell those to raise funds.  I did not have time to do that this time, so I just did this in desperation.

Again, thank you for your donations, prayers, goodwill, shares and just for thinking of people far away, that are never seen or heard.
