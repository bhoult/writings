# I awoke to good tidings of great joy.  It was snowing in Russellville!  Finally.

I awoke to good tidings of great joy.  It was snowing in Russellville!  Finally.  My weather app concurred so I scrambled from the warmth of my bed and rushed to the door.

Nothing.  Mud.  Cold mud.

The cats immediately started whining about food.

I proclaimed "this shall not pass!" And decided to drive up Nebo.  I will not fail!

And here I am on Nebo.  

Nothing.  Lies.  Betrayal.

Et tu 2021....
