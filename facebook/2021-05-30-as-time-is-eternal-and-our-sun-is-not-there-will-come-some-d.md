# As time is eternal, and our sun is not, there will come some distant evening tha

As time is eternal, and our sun is not, there will come some distant evening that it sets for the final time.  There is some comfort that man, fragile as we are, will have long since been relieved of its long duty to witness such things.  So that final flickering light will dim unnoticed and unmourned.
