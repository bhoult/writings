# The Question

The Question

And here lies another failure.  Here follows the shame of recognizing that what I struggled for so long to overcome existed in my mind alone.  That once again, lack of respect for the question has lead to only imagined success.  A temporary distraction from an unacknowledgable complexity I dare not consider.  

And so I pretend that it is simple.  A straw man, an effigy, an idol, a caricature.  These things can be made to dance at will.  As can I.

I said that I "knew" when I had only grown tired of looking for nuance.  I said I "believed" to give my preference an imagined reality.  I said I "understood" in order to silence new information.  I said it was finished when it had barely begun.

Because the road only becomes longer as it is traveled, while life only becomes shorter.  Because the ghosts on the path made the same mistakes and have little to offer.

And so with hyperbole and generalization I did not respect the question and thus solved only imagined problems to achieve so many meaningless victories.

That which was broken, remains broken.  That which was hidden is hidden still.
