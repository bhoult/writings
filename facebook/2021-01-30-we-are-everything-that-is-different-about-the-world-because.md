# We are everything that is different about the world because we existed. 

We are everything that is different about the world because we existed. 

Your thoughts are potential futures, your experience is your present, your memory is your past. You live as a vector passing through these points.

Imagine a pencil sitting on a table. You move it a foot to the left. The pencil is not you, but it's movement is you. If you did not exist it would still be where it was. 

You are not other people, but they are changed because of you. The change is you, the difference in them is who you were. Likewise other people will make you different than what you would have been had you never met. They are not you, but neither are you what you were. You are changed, each moment. Something new.

There is no demarcation except that which you imagine. You are drawing a circle upon the water of an endless ocean and asking is this me? Where do I begin and end? Why does the circle fade and the ripples spread into the distance?

You are eternal. Your body will decay, your thoughts grow silent, but the impression you make is the foundation on which the future is formed. All of history conspired to produce who you are in this moment.  An unbroken chain from the first thought, bound together by the lives of each of your ancestors. By their thoughts driving the actions which we now look back upon to judge their worth.

And so we will be judged.  By what we made different, by the threads we bound or cut.

We are not the future. But it will be what it is because of who we are now. It will be changed by every choice we make, every action will echo through eternity. Nothing stands alone, independent whole and complete. 

We are what is different about the world because we were in it.
