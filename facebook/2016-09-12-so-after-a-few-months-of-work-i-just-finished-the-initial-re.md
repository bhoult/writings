# So... after a few months of work I just finished the initial release of a new CM

So... after a few months of work I just finished the initial release of a new CMS (content management system).  The core is open source and I will be trying to sell themes to support further development.

You can see it running at http://ajaxcms.org

Basically it is a client side CMS that uses static files on the backend.    It is similar in function to stuff like wordpress, joomla, or drupal but it works in almost the opposite way that all the current CMS systems operate.  There is a better explanation on the site.

For the not technically inclined... it is supposed to make it easier to create fancy looking websites that are faster and more secure than the current offerings.
