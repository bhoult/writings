# There is an anger when you notice that others are  unconstrained by the rules yo

There is an anger when you notice that others are  unconstrained by the rules you were taught were absolute.  Some kind of dismay that God's punishment wasn't timely enough.   We become inpatient for  retribution.   

Perhaps we do unto others what we know should have been done to us, and we decide justice is ours rather than God's.  So we close our eyes to throw the first stone.
