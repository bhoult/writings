# Last night I dreamt I was a giant toad inflated like a balloon, floating cloud-l

Last night I dreamt I was a giant toad inflated like a balloon, floating cloud-like serenely over Athens.  I keep hearing a voice saying "you are not a cloud... Come down you fool".  I keep responding "I am not a toad, I can't come down."

I have been thinking about it all day.
