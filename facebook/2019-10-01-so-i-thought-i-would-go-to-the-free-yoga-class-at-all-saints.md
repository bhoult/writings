# So I thought I would go to the free yoga class at All Saints Episcopal Church to

So I thought I would go to the free yoga class at All Saints Episcopal Church tonight.  The rather undignified sweat soaked specimen in the picture is the result of a one hour ass kicking by a bunch of kindly older ladies.  They looked on in concern as I slid about on my soggy mat trying not to fall in a rapidly forming pool of sweat.  Which I did multiple times.  All this from trying to assume some awkward position and merely not move for 30 seconds or so.

If your ego could benefit from the loss of a few pounds, or if your humility needs a little work you should come next time.  They have it every Tuesday at 5:30pm.  If nothing else you can just laugh at me.

Next time I will bring a towel.  And a mop, and a bucket.  Probably a squeegee.
