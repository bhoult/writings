# So this just happened: About 15 minutes ago I heard someone walking around on my

So this just happened: About 15 minutes ago I heard someone walking around on my deck.  I was expecting them to knock, but I did not hear anything.  I opened the door and there was a bear about 15 feet away.  We looked at each other for a bit.  I thought he was pretty small, maybe just a little over 100lbs.  He seemed similarly unimpressed with me.  I went in to get my camera and he had wandered down the hill.  This is the best picture I could get.
