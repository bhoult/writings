# There was a tornado at my house a few years ago.  Thick clouds, heavy rain.  And

There was a tornado at my house a few years ago.  Thick clouds, heavy rain.  And then for a few minutes it suddenly stopped and the sun broke through a hole in the clouds. For a few minutes everything glowed in strange light.  I managed a few random pictures before the rain returned.
