# I tried to get some coffee a little while ago.  He did not understand "large lat

I tried to get some coffee a little while ago.  He did not understand "large latte" and hopefully showed me a tiny little cup.  I waved my hands about trying to indicate a bigger cup.  He came back with a single shot of espresso in the bottom inch of a decent sized cup.

I tried to explain that I wanted him to fill it up.  He left shaking his head and mumbling something in Greek.

And that is how I just drank 12 shots of espresso.  I think I may be having heart palpitations.
